**Why a redo (Alan):** "a glue stick or a pencil is really small, so how could it be a building? The look is that
children turned classroom objects into buildings and made the buildings from things in the classroom." So every
building below is something kids BUILT out of MANY classroom objects shown at their REAL size, never one small object
made giant. Scale: a school desk top is 330 studs wide, so 1 stud is about 4 mm. Real sizes in studs: a pencil about
48 long; a ruler about 75 long; a craft stick about 28 long; an index card about 32 x 20; a paper cup about 25 tall;
a juice box about 10 x 15 x 25; a small paint pot about 15 across; a tissue box about 60 x 30 x 30; a pencil tin
about 50 x 20 x 13; a ring binder about 80 tall; a shoebox about 85 x 50 x 30. So a 250-stud tower is many objects
stacked: count them right. Keep it chunky and readable, like the good ones already made (look at
`assets/models/porthole-book-tower/concept-front.png`, `assets/models/shoebox-hall/concept-front.png`,
