# First plane concept options

Option A, classic dart: sharp triangular wings, a long pointed nose and a narrow folded keel make this the most recognisable paper-plane silhouette. Overlapping paper tabs define the body, nose, one-piece wings and tail; two small pellet tubes sit in taped paper cradles below the wing roots.

Option B, chunky glider: broader trapezoidal wings, thicker folded leading edges, upright wingtip folds and a shorter nose give the starter plane a sturdier toy shape. It keeps the same four-part assembly and paired strapped pellet tubes, with a wider span than A.

Option C, engineered fold: a doubled cardstock spine, layered wing roots and isolated straight tape tabs make the joins more visible. The exploded view shows a connected wing saddle as one removable piece, alongside the separate body, nose, tail and pellet tube.

Every sheet includes matching red and blue three-quarter views, a red side view, a red top view and a small exploded view. Red wings use solid round stickers; blue wings use solid five-point stars. No text or additional symbols appear on the sheets. These are concept pictures, not dimensionally exact model drawings.
