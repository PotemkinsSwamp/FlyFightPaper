# Skins: same hitbox, four tiers, team outline

**Decided:** 2026-11-13 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force; ideas, tier names and the way shape skins are made are still being talked through on the
[skins plan](../plan/SKINS-PLAN.md). Nothing is made yet.

## Decision
- **A skin never changes the hitbox.** It is only a look, wrapped as tightly round the plane's real shape as possible,
  with no hitbox of its own, so wearing one is never an advantage or a disadvantage. This replaces "the skin draws
  over the whole plane" in the [shop plan](2026-10-18-shop-plan.md).
- **Tiers, at least four** (like common, rare, legendary, mythical, but with classroom names). The tier sets the price,
  the effort to earn it, and where it can be a reward. The top tiers change the plane's form (dragon, bat, origami
  crane) and come with a matching trail; lower tiers only change the look on the plane's own shape.
- **Earning:** the lower two tiers can be earned. The top two can only be bought, except later through things that
  keep players coming back: a pass like Fortnite's, the playtime calendar, weekly missions, or being among the top
  players.
- **Team games: no team colours on skins.** A team shows through three signs: a faint red or blue outline on the
  plane, the name in team colour, and the square or circle under the name (made easier to see from far away). Aim
  help already never locks onto a teammate.
- **One skin for all your planes** if the hitbox problem can be solved for every part mix; otherwise a skin is tied
  to a body.
- **Bots wear skins,** so players see them in matches and want them.
- **Ideas kept:** shark jet, dragon, newspaper glider, gold foil (not star stickers: tacky), homework A+, comic book,
  origami crane (if its shape can match the hitbox), candy wrapper, old treasure map, bat, sticker bomb. More needed.

## Why
Alan: skins that change hitboxes are a big problem in many games and must not be one here. Tiers let prices and
effort be set fairly and let skins be rewards. Names, the dot and aim help already tell teams apart, and some skins
change the whole plane, so team paint isn't needed.
