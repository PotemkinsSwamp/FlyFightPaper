# Touch controls before launch, controller after

**Decided:** 2026-10-23 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
- Phones and tablets get full touch controls **before** the Stage 11 private playtest, not after launch as
  [VISION](../vision/VISION.md) first said; controller support comes right after touch.
- The [touch plan](../plan/TOUCH-PLAN.md) is approved: a steering stick by default, with "Drag to aim" as a Settings
  option; a real-phone check through a private publish once the Studio steps are done.
- Touch players get more aim help, but **only after research**: "it has to be tuned", so how other games do it and
  how to measure it come first.

## Why
Most Roblox players are on phones, every mouse-only screen built now adds to the redo, and the Stage 11 playtest's
friends may be on phones (the touch plan's reasons, which Alan agreed with).
