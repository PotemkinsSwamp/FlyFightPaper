# The winners' dark is a curtain, their spotlight a cone for show

**Decided:** 2026-11-20 (Alan)  
**Status:** built; Alan has run it in Studio ("it's good now", framing "good now"); the last camera change was seen
by him, not by Claude

## Decision
- **No real lights on the winners.** "The spotlight can't just be a false light and we just light the model up
  through artificial means?" The winners stand in the room's ordinary light; the spotlight is for show.
- **The dark is a curtain.** One nearly black, slightly see-through sheet behind the winners; the room's own
  lighting is left alone.
- **The spotlight is a real cone, not a flat picture.** "We kind of want a cone that actually kind of fully
  envelops the character." He picked strength A (24 very faint nested cones) from a Studio preview of three.
- **The cone must not change how the winners look.** "Make the cone artificial light. Give the models the same level
  of lighting that we had before." Its haze may not lie over them.
- **The light stays on the desk.** "See how the lighting kind of extends over the desk. That shouldn't happen."
- **The winners stand further back on their desks,** not on the front edge.
- **The winners are in place before the camera moves.** "When the camera arrives, they're already in their spot."
- **Every winner is in their light,** a tall avatar too.
- **The camera sits a little further back,** so 2nd and 3rd are well inside the view.

## Why
Real spotlights on the winners came on late and pulsed, in three different tries. Alan played each cut and said what
was wrong: the [playtest record](../playtests/2026-11-20-winners-curtain-and-cone.md).
