# Four more runner-up emotes, to match the eight for 1st place

**Decided:** 2026-11-24 (Alan, after looking at what exists)
**Status:** Decided. The four shot cards are written and wait for his OK; nothing is made.

## Decision
- **As many runner-up emotes as 1st-place ones** ("We need to make equal amounts of runner-ups ... we need 8 runner-up emotes as well"). Eight exist for 1st (Toy Pilot, Camera Flick, Book Kicker, Bubblegum, Magician, A+, Skeet Shoot, Fold It Up); four for the runner-ups (Good Game, Nap, Next One, So Close).
- **The next four:** [Sore Loser](../workshop/emotes/sore-loser.md), [Doodler](../workshop/emotes/doodler.md), [Sneaky Toss](../workshop/emotes/sneaky-toss.md), [Test Flight](../workshop/emotes/test-flight.md) ("Let's do sore loser, doodler. Sneaky toss. And test flight").
- **Slow Clap is dropped** (Claude's advice, his "let's go with your idea"): Good Game is already three slow claps and a lazy wave, so it would be the free emote sold again.
- **Sneaky Toss's ball misses** ("Let's just make the ball like it misses, so it doesn't fall short. It just like goes off to the side behind them"). Claude's reading: it flies past 1st, behind them, and off to the far side; it never hits 1st, so it need not be fitted to each of the eight 1st-place emotes.

## Why
- The two slots should offer the same choice once emotes are sold.
- The four add feelings the runner-ups lack: jealous, daydreaming, cheeky, playful (the first four: gracious, bored, already moving on, sad).
