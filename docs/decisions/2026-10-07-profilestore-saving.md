# Saving uses ProfileStore

**Decided:** 2026-10-07 (Alan, D3)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
Player saves use the ProfileStore library.

## Why
It is the standard, tested Roblox library for safe saving (session locks, autosave), instead of our own code.
