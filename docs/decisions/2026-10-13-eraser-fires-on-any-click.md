# The eraser fires on any click

**Decided:** 2026-10-13 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
The guided eraser fires straight if its lock isn't finished and seeks once locked. Waiting for the lock is the smart play, never forced. Bots still wait for a full lock.

## Why
Every weapon should fire the instant you click.
