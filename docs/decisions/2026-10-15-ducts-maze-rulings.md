# Ceiling ducts: a dark maze, a real pipe, air you fly against

**Decided:** 2026-10-15 (Alan, after seeing the first duct build in Studio)
**Status:** in force

## Decision
- Fewer vents (about four), at irregular spots, not a symmetrical pattern. The ducts work like a maze: enter at any
  vent, leave at any other, with some planning needed to find the way.
- The narrow branch is a small round pipe the whole way through, not a slot that opens back into a normal duct. Fly
  it straight or you don't make it.
- Dark inside, but you can still see. No lamps: the light comes up from below, from the classroom's ceiling lights
  the ducts pass over.
- Fan blades must visibly spin. Fans blow air at you: flying against the air slows you down (easier to time the
  blades); flying with it speeds you up. The blower works the same way.
- Second look: fans a little slower than once a second and much harsher (75% of health a hit); flying against the
  air was a little too slow (now half the effect of flying with it); blades must stay inside their duct, in a housing
  that looks fixed to the duct.
- Design pass (next): HVAC duct walls with metal textures from real HVAC references, fans that look like real duct
  fans, the pipe like plain HVAC plumbing (not rusted), plus: cobwebs, plastic toy spiders, lost toys and tools.

## Why
Vents in a grid don't look like real vents, and a gate that opens straight back up isn't a pipe. A dark maze with
air to fight makes the ducts an escape you have to learn.
