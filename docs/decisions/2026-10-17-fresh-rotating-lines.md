# Game words stay fresh: rotating lines for events

**Decided:** 2026-10-17 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
- Messages that describe something happening in the game (your plane going down, refolding, landing, folds, round
  events and the like) come from a small set of short, on-theme paper lines picked at random, not one fixed sentence.
- Waits are described ("your plane is being refolded"), not counted in seconds.
- Words are for a young audience: plain and easy to read. Example: the hangar's "Clear mount" becomes
  "Clear weapon spot" (the button stays; clicking the weapon again also clears it).

## Why
Alan: other games vary their lines, and it keeps the game feeling fresh and fun; one fixed sentence every time feels
flat. Kids must understand every button without help.
