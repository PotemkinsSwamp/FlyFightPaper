# Shop direction: a school store counter, a starter pack with a real timer

**Decided:** 2026-11-11 (Alan, after the [shop research](../research/SHOP-RESEARCH-2026-11-11.md))
**Status:** decided; nothing built (next: a concept picture for Alan's OK)

## Decision
- **Research first** (Alan): Roblox buying habits, what players like, the rules for kids, and how to make it tempting,
  all turned into our classroom style. Done: [shop research](../research/SHOP-RESEARCH-2026-11-11.md).
- **What the shop is:** the paid shop is a **school store window / counter** (a big "this week" poster, price tags on
  string, things that wiggle and pop). Plus a **sticker album** that shows what you own (full-colour sticker) and
  what's next (pencil sketch), and a **prize box** with a free daily gift.
- **Starter pack: yes, with a real 3-day timer** (Roblox's own recipe: one purchase only, about 99 R$, 2-5 things, a
  big discount, a small HUD button while it runs). The timer is real time and never resets or comes back
  ([Roblox's rule](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization)); no pop-up after a loss.
- **Next step:** straight to a concept picture of the shop in our style (Codex picture), no picture board first.
- Unchanged: prices in the [shop prices plan](../plan/SHOP-PRICES-PLAN.md); offers only for the player's own goal,
  capped, never after a loss or a death, calm words; the research's checklist runs before any design is shown.

## Why
- Alan: the shop must look tempting ("a carrot on a stick"), with movement and feedback, but made in the classroom.
- The school store counter fits plane parts and gives a featured poster spot; the album and prize box are the
  research's best fits for "what you own" and a free reason to visit.
- Roblox reports starter packs as its best-evidenced offer (2-10% more revenue), with the timer part of its recipe.
