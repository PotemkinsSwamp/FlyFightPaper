# Shop direction: a Roblox shop window in our materials, a starter pack with a real timer

**Decided:** 2026-11-11 (Alan, after the [shop research](../research/SHOP-RESEARCH-2026-11-11.md))
**Status:** decided; the store counter look was replaced the same night (below); nothing built

## Change, same night: a normal Roblox shop window in our materials
- Alan turned down the store-counter concept (a counter with jars, bags and a sticker-album book): "completely
  unrecognizable from all other roblox uis". He showed shops from other games (screenshots in
  `.planning/shop-concept/roblox-examples/`, not in git).
- **The shop is laid out like the shops players know:** a window over the game with a title bar and a big red X,
  a scrolling body with section headings (Featured, Passes, Paper, Boosts), bright cards with a glow behind one big
  icon, the amount on a dark label, a big green Robux buy button, value stickers that climb ("Good Value", "Most
  Value"), big icon tabs beside the window, and movement (shines, hue shifts, bobbing icons). Our paper, crayon and
  classroom materials go on top; the layout stays familiar.
- **Next: a personalised shop, research first** (Alan 2026-11-12): "it needs to have an algorithm": the shop changes
  for the player looking at it (spenders vs. players who haven't spent), timers and rotations change. Research brief:
  how personalised shops and offers work (mobile games, Roblox), what Roblox and the law allow. A broad search that
  includes **personal discounts** (not decided; Alan: "whether we apply them or not is a different question") and
  **offers after a fold or a lost match** (research, then Alan decides; the Nov 11 "never after a loss" rule stands until
  then). Research done: [personal shop research](../research/PERSONAL-SHOP-RESEARCH-2026-11-12.md) (options A-P
  for Alan's picks).
- **Starter pack contents so far** (Alan 2026-11-12): Swept Wings, 1,500 Paper and **1 hour of 2x XP** (not 2x Paper);
  worth 227 R$ bought separately; its price (99 R$ in the preview) still needs his OK.
- **"Limited time!"** is fine on the starter pack (its timer is real). **Gift to a friend** buttons are wanted
  beside the buy buttons (a new feature: plan it before building).
- The store counter, the sticker album and the prize box below are replaced by this; the free daily gift stays as a
  "Free gift" card in the shop.

## Decision (first version)
- **Research first** (Alan): Roblox buying habits, what players like, the rules for kids, and how to make it tempting,
  all turned into our classroom style. Done: [shop research](../research/SHOP-RESEARCH-2026-11-11.md).
- **What the shop is:** the paid shop is a **school store window / counter** (a big "this week" poster, price tags on
  string, things that wiggle and pop). Plus a **sticker album** that shows what you own (full-colour sticker) and
  what's next (pencil sketch), and a **prize box** with a free daily gift.
- **Starter pack: yes, with a real 3-day timer** (Roblox's own recipe: one purchase only, about 99 R$, 2-5 things, a
  big discount, a small HUD button while it runs). The timer is real time and never resets or comes back
  ([Roblox's rule](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization)); no pop-up after a loss.
- **Audience** (Alan): the main audience is probably **not children**, so don't tune everything to kids (looks, words,
  difficulty). The platform rules still apply to everyone (Roblox's monetization rules, no fake urgency, no random
  paid items), and younger players will be there too, so the fair-play checklist stays.
- **Next step:** straight to a concept picture of the shop in our style (Codex picture), no picture board first.
- Unchanged: prices in the [shop prices plan](../plan/SHOP-PRICES-PLAN.md); offers only for the player's own goal,
  capped, never after a loss or a death, calm words; the research's checklist runs before any design is shown.

## Why
- Alan: the shop must look tempting ("a carrot on a stick"), with movement and feedback, but made in the classroom.
- The school store counter fits plane parts and gives a featured poster spot; the album and prize box are the
  research's best fits for "what you own" and a free reason to visit.
- Roblox reports starter packs as its best-evidenced offer (2-10% more revenue), with the timer part of its recipe.
