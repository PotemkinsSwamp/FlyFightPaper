# The Range is its own small map; the Hangar Tour happens there

**Decided:** 2026-10-19 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force (design and research next; nothing built yet)

## Decision
- **The Range is a separate small map**, not the classroom: wide open, your plane held still (it can only slide side
  to side), flat 2D paper-plane targets hung on fishing wire from metal rigs, moving in circles, figure eights and
  random paths, so you still lead your shots. A speed picker: Noob / Challenge / Veteran / Maneuvers. Weapons can be
  switched quickly.
- **Alan's picture of the Range (later the same day; replaces the wires above):** it feels like a big kid-made puppet theatre stage (Alan: not a cardboard box, too small; a paper curtain behind, warm stage lights, cardboard cut-outs of our town's buildings drawn on as scenery with crayon clouds, paper curtains at the sides; the theatre fills the whole view: no floor, room or windows, only the theatre and the Range's menu). Big and deep: close cut-outs to see how fast each weapon tears them, far ones near the scenery for leading and range, lots of open air to swoop in; your plane small in the view. A cut-out tears more with each hit (several steps) before it splits, falls and bursts.
  The targets are flat 2D paper-plane cut-outs on sticks coming up from below, as if a child hidden behind the box
  were moving them (no child or hand is ever seen): back and forth, up and down; at the side of the box the stick
  turns and the cut-out flips to face the other way. Hit enough, a target rips in half like torn paper, the halves fall,
  its stick drops out of sight and a new one comes up from below. Your plane stays put; you see it from the wings up
  (its weapons in view) and can move the view left, right, up and down and shoot freely. The plane can be fully
  customised there (any body, any weapons: e.g. the toilet-roll body with 10 capacity and 6 rubber band guns).
- **The Range is not a tutorial.** It can be opened any time: it is the Training board's fourth card, in Match Tips'
  place (Alan, later the same day; no tick, it isn't a lesson). Inside, sticky notes just point at things and say what
  they do ("click this to do this").
- **Match Tips leaves the board:** they just show up in your first matches, switched off in Settings (maybe a list of
  tips somewhere later).
- **The Training board** looks like concept B (`assets/references/ui-concepts/training-board-b.png`, then -c with the
  Range): a cork board, the title on a taped torn sheet, each card a pinned sheet of coloured paper with its Play strip
  under it. No "Coming soon" or greyed-out cards: everything on it is being built now.
- **The Hangar Tour is the tutorial**, and it runs inside the Range map: it walks you through building a plane and
  setting up weapons, then hands over to the Range and its sticky notes.
- **Leaving a match warns you.** Clicking a lesson or the Range from a match first says you'll leave your game
  for another server.
- Flight School and Combat School stay in the classroom copy (approved; they need the real map).
- The Training Room's 10-minute limit is dropped (Claude's suggestion, Alan agreed: people leave or are kicked for being idle; Roblox closes empty rooms itself, and a timer
  could cut off a slow learner).
- Research first ([range and hangar research](../research/RANGE-HANGAR-RESEARCH-2026-10-19.md)): how shooter and flight games do ranges, target patterns, difficulty, hangars and quick loadout
  switching.

## Why
Testing a weapon in the big map means chasing a plane down first; a range lets you just shoot and see how each weapon
works, like the ranges in other shooters.
