# The Range is its own small map; the Hangar Tour happens there

**Decided:** 2026-10-19 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force (design and research next; nothing built yet)

## Decision
- **The Range is a separate small map**, not the classroom: wide open, your plane held still (it can only slide side
  to side), flat 2D paper-plane targets hung on fishing wire from metal rigs, moving in circles, figure eights and
  random paths, so you still lead your shots. A speed picker: Noob / Challenge / Veteran / Maneuvers. Weapons can be
  switched quickly.
- **The Range is not a tutorial.** It has its own button in the menu and can be opened any time. Inside, sticky notes
  just point at things and say what they do ("click this to do this").
- **The Hangar Tour is the tutorial**, and it runs inside the Range map: it walks you through building a plane and
  setting up weapons, then hands over to the Range and its sticky notes.
- **Leaving a match warns you.** Clicking Tutorial (a lesson) or Range from a match first says you'll leave your game
  for another server.
- Flight School and Combat School stay in the classroom copy (approved; they need the real map).
- Proposed, waiting on Alan's OK: drop the Training Room's 10-minute limit (Claude's suggestion; Roblox closes empty rooms itself, and a timer
  could cut off a slow learner).
- Research first ([range and hangar research](../research/RANGE-HANGAR-RESEARCH-2026-10-19.md)): how shooter and flight games do ranges, target patterns, difficulty, hangars and quick loadout
  switching.

## Why
Testing a weapon in the big map means chasing a plane down first; a range lets you just shoot and see how each weapon
works, like the ranges in other shooters.
