# Points: folds, assists by damage share, zone pay, bonuses

**Decided:** 2026-10-14 (Alan's points workshop)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
A knockout is a fold (100). Assists pay by damage share (10-80). In Airspace, the zone pays 20 per 5 s and folds outside the zone pay 40%. Streak +25, revenge +25, first fold +100, paper targets 50. Every point pops up on screen. Paper and XP per point were rescaled so a typical match pays the same.

## Why
Points should reward playing the objective and good teamwork, and every point should feel earned on screen.
