# A boost prize a "2x forever" pass covers pays 2,000 Paper an hour instead

**Decided:** 2026-11-25 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
A player who owns a "2x forever" pass can't use a timed boost of that kind. When the playtime map or the daily gift
would give one, they get Paper instead: 2,000 Paper for each hour of boost (`playtime.boost_covered_paper_per_hour` in
`config/economy.json`; the daily gift's 15-minute boosts pay 500, `daily_gift.boost_covered_paper`, was 250).

Alan, on the first number (1,000 an hour): "isnt the 1000 paper kind of low? its punishing players for spending robux."
He agreed to 2,000 and to the daily gift using the same rate ("yes to all three").

## Why
At 1,000 an hour a player with both passes got 2,000 Paper from the treasure chest's boost card, less than the 3,000
Paper card beside it. At 2,000 it pays 4,000: buying a pass is never a loss and gets a small thank-you.
