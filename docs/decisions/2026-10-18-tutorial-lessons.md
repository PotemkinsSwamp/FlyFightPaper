# Tutorial: short separate lessons, fixed training plane, always replayable

**Decided:** 2026-10-18 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
- The tutorial follows the [tutorial plan](../plan/TUTORIAL-PLAN.md): separate step-by-step lessons (Flight School,
  Combat Training, Weapons Range, Hangar Tour, Match Tips), done the way most games do it.
- Lessons use a **fixed training plane**, not the player's own build.
- A new player goes **Flight School, then Combat Training, then plays**. Each can be skipped, and the game must
  **tell them** they can always come back to it from the menu.
- Keep it **short**: 15 minutes is too long, people would leave. (Claude's pick: lessons about 2 and 3 minutes, Training
  Room closes after 8 minutes.)
- **Match Tips** can be turned off in Settings.

## Why
Alan: a tutorial must teach in steps (do this, look at this) and cover everything, including plane building, but
not drag on or people leave. The earlier build told players to shoot targets that weren't there.
