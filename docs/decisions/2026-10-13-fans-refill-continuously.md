# Fans give Lift for as long as you stay in them

**Decided:** 2026-10-13 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
An upward fan adds Lift every second you are in its gust, on every pass, with no timer, and pushes you up hard like a real fan. Staying alive forever using fans is fine.

## Why
The old fan gave about 20 Lift once and nothing on a second pass, which felt broken.
