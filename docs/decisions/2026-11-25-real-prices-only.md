# Long play earns it, impatient players can pay; crossed-out prices are always real

**Decided:** 2026-11-25 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
Alan: "our game needs to be structured in a way where longer playtime is rewarded in a way where you don't really have
to spend Robux, and then shorter playtime drives the player to want to spend Robux ... We need to reward the long-term
player, incentivize the impatient player by giving them methods to pay impatiently."

He suggested showing made-up "was" prices slashed to a fake discount (a starter pack shown at "75% off" a price it never
had). Claude declined to build that: a made-up earlier price is against US, EU and UK pricing law, Roblox's own
guidance says a discount isn't genuine if an item is always "on sale" at the same price
([Roblox monetization](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization)), and studies and regulators are
looking at exactly this in kids' Roblox games in 2026. The honest versions were offered instead, and Alan agreed: **"yes
use the real prices, chest at 4 hours"**.

- A crossed-out price is only ever a real one: what the same things cost bought one by one at today's prices, or an
  item's normal price during a real, limited sale after it has sold at that price.
- The starter pack shows 218 R$ crossed out against its 99 R$ price: Swept Wings 49 + 1,500 Paper 99 + 1 hour 2x XP 25
  + a Paper chest 45, added up by the shop from the live prices (it showed a hand-typed 272 before, left over from older
  prices; only in Studio practice mode).
- Impatient players get honest shortcuts: unlock a part now for Robux or play N more matches, boosts that get a goal
  N matches sooner, Double this match, a chest now instead of at the end of the playtime chart.
- The playtime chart's treasure chest comes at 4 hours of play in a day ([playtime rewards plan](../plan/PLAYTIME-REWARDS-PLAN.md)); replaced the same day by 2 hours ([decision](2026-11-25-playtime-chart-two-hours.md)).

## Why
Players who play a lot should be able to earn everything without paying; players who want it sooner pay to skip the
wait. Lying about prices to kids risks the game's page and its earnings, and isn't needed: real bundle totals give the
same slashed-price look.
