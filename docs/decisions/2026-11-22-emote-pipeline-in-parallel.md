# Emotes are made by a six-step pipeline, several at once in side sessions

**Decided:** 2026-11-22 (Alan, after Magician took about six and a half hours: "we cant be doing 2 every 8 hours we need to figure out how to speed this up")
**Status:** Written down; the four side sessions started the same day.

## Decision
- **The work runs side by side:** one side session per remaining emote ("We need to create some subsessions for creating everything else").
- **One fixed order for every emote** (his words: "direction, approval of direction, creating the Gemini, making the sound at the same time so we don't have to recreate the sound later ... then creating it in Roblox, then applying the sound to the Roblox one, and then a final review"). The steps and who does what: [emote pipeline](../workshop/emote-pipeline.md).
- **The sound is made with the reference video**, not after the look is approved.
- **Claude's calls inside it, his to reverse:** the four emotes started are So Close, Skeet Shoot, A+ and Fold It Up; each emote lives in its own files so two sessions never write the same file; Studio's Play, the recordings and the uploads stay with the primary session; the side sessions run on Opus.

## Why
- On Magician the body took minutes; the hours went on the ending being rebuilt three times after he saw it, and on the sound being made in four rounds after the look was done ([the log](../workshop/emote-making.md)).
- Several emotes can wait on his look at the same time instead of one after another.
