# Winners' circle redo: a dramatic scene, and emotes sold by place

**Decided:** 2026-11-19 (Alan)  
**Status:** direction set; the scene and the full emote list are in the [plan](../plan/WINNERS-CIRCLE-PLAN.md), still being talked through; nothing built

## Decision
- **Reference:** two clips Alan likes from another Roblox game (its winner's moment and its shop). What he wants from
  it: a little cinema for 1st, music that goes with the emote, then back out to 2nd and 3rd; in our style, not a copy.
  Its build: about 12 s; black, then the winner alone on a small stage, one move at the camera, a held pose while name
  signs come in, a wipe to the vote. Its shop sells win animations, win backgrounds and win music separately.
- **The scene is the problem first:** hopping up one at a time, a small zoom out, random dances and confetti is boring.
- **Dramatic start:** the room's lights dim, the winners are already on their desks, a spotlight comes on.
- **No random dances, and not everyone doing the same thing.** Each winner plays their own emote. Dances can be sold
  later as emotes too.
- **Emotes are sold by place:** 1st-place emotes, and 2nd/3rd-place emotes.
- **Alan's 1st-place ideas:** flick the plane at the camera, it fills the screen and knocks the camera over (sold,
  never the default); kick the stack of books over, make a mess, pump the arms, then throw a book at the camera;
  a celebration dance that throws confetti at the camera (covers the view, doesn't knock it over).
- **Alan's 2nd/3rd-place ideas:** sitting on the books folding a new paper plane; sitting on the books shaking the
  head, sad not to be 1st.
- **Liveliness comes from a toy, not camera work** (his example: a shooter's winner who drives an RC car around): the
  kid playing with their plane like a toy plane.
- **The class may react** (things thrown in from the other desks), with care for where each thing comes from.
- **Plane emotes** (tricks in flight) go on the [ideas page](../plan/IDEAS.md).
- **Music:** licensed only; a short clip of a real song is not allowed just because it is short.

## Why
Alan: the end of a round should feel like an event and be something players want to buy into; today's is generic.
