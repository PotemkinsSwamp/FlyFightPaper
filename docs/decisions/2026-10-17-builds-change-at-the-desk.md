# Builds change at the desk: Edit from the quick hangar, "Save & fly" in the hangar

**Decided:** 2026-10-17 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
- **One list of five builds**, the same everywhere. The desk (quick hangar) is the one place your plane changes.
- The quick hangar shows the **picked build's** picture and stats, and an **Edit** button that opens the full
  hangar on that build (no more Menu → Hangar hunting; the plane is parked at the desk, so it's safe).
- In the full hangar opened from the desk: two clearly different buttons, **Save** and **Save & fly – 120 Paper**
  (saves, refolds, back to the desk with the new plane). From the main menu, saving makes that build the one you
  fly next. Opened from pause while flying: Save only, with "Saved. Land at your desk to fly it."
- A refold still costs **120 Paper**; refolding into the build you already fly is not charged (nothing changes).
- The hangar opens on the build you fly, asks before throwing away unsaved edits, and both screens always
  highlight the same build.
- The tutorial points at **Edit** the first time a new player lands at the desk.

## Why
Alan: new players won't know to go Menu → Hangar mid-match, or whether edits reach the quick hangar. Games with
saved loadouts put Edit next to Select (Call of Duty class select, Battlefield 2042 deploy screen, War Robots
decks), keep one list of builds everywhere (Battlefield 2042's two editors confused players), and make "pick" and
"save over" look different (Hunt: Showdown). Research notes: [playtest 2026-10-17](../playtests/2026-10-17.md).
