# Takeoff is automatic

**Decided:** 2026-10-13 (Alan, D11)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
The plane rolls down its desk runway, off the edge and through the paper-chain hoop by itself; the player takes control once it is through the hoop.

## Why
A clean, reliable start every life, with no fiddly launch.
