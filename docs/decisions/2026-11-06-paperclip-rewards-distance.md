# The paperclip rewards long shots; Tab shows live standings

**Decided:** 2026-11-06 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force; Alan's Studio check to come

## Decision
- Paperclips stay extremely fast and precise. Speed and accuracy are not what gets cut.
- Two paperclips take turns; they never fire together. Each has a 1.75 s reload.
- Damage depends on how far the shot flew: half the labelled 24 up close, more than the label far away. Claude's numbers
  to tune: 12 at 150 studs or less, 24 at 500, 36 at 1000, 60 at 2400 (the shot's reach), in straight lines between.
  The lead ring reaches as far as the shot for this gun, so the top of that scale can be aimed at.
- Tab opens and closes the live standings (press, not hold), over the game, updating as scores change. On a phone, a tap
  on the score strip does the same. The Menu button and Esc open the menu; Roblox's player list stays off.

- A paperclip aimed at you glints, like a sniper's scope: a real, bright flash on their plane when in view; when not, a
  really faint reddish glint spread along the bottom of the screen, not an arrow. No sound.

## Why
- In play two clips folded most planes in two hits, because each hit was 48 at once and almost never missed. On paper
  pellets do more damage per second, but they miss a lot.
- A marksman's reward should be for the hard shot: the further away, the more skill, the more damage.
