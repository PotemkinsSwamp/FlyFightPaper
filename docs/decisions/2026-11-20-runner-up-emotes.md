# The first three runner-up emotes: Good Game, Nap, Next One

**Decided:** 2026-11-20 (Alan)
**Status:** the first Good Game (Dev panel "2nd/3rd") was not accepted by Alan in Play ("stiff ... too fast ...
unnatural"); Nap and Next One have their raw body moves and are not built

## Decision
- The three runner-up emotes as described on the [emote making](../workshop/emote-making.md) page ("Runner-up
  emotes: shot cards"): Alan, "yes to all".
- **Already in their starting pose in the dark**: the light finds a kid who is already sitting.
- **Sitting is cross-legged on top of the stack**, so it looks the same on 2nd's four books and 3rd's two.
- **Nap wakes up for 1st**: when 1st's light clicks on, the napper startles awake for a moment and dozes off again.
- His go for the nine generator tries (each uploads a private animation to his account) and for Edit-mode screen
  grabs.
- Earlier the same day: the winners' shot scales to the screen (as close as the screen allows, across and up and
  down); "ok its good". Details on the [modes and scoring](../systems/modes-and-scoring.md) page.

## Why
Runner-ups get something of their own to do that stays small beside 1st's emote. Sitting already in the dark avoids
a standing shape dropping into a sit at the click.
