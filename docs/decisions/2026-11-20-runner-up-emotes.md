# The first three runner-up emotes: Good Game, Nap, Next One

**Decided:** 2026-11-20 (Alan)
**Status:** the first Good Game (Dev panel "2nd/3rd") was not accepted by Alan in Play ("stiff ... too fast ...
unnatural"); its remake is approved by Alan in Play ("the slow clap is good now", "the stance is good", "the
wave is fine"), Studio only until its clips are uploaded; Nap is approved by Alan in Play ("I think that's good. I think we move on"; one kid's arm glitch
found and fixed: "arms look fine now"), Studio only until its two clips are uploaded; Next One is built; after his first look in Play ("It's fine", but fiddling) it was rebuilt to his four runs of the hand and is approved by him in Play ("it looks good move on"), Studio only until its clip is uploaded

## Decision
- The three runner-up emotes as described on the [emote making](../workshop/emote-making.md) page ("Runner-up
  emotes: shot cards"): Alan, "yes to all".
- **Already in their starting pose in the dark**: the light finds a kid who is already sitting.
- **Sitting is cross-legged on top of the stack**, so it looks the same on 2nd's four books and 3rd's two.
- **Nap wakes up for 1st**: when 1st's light clicks on, the napper startles awake for a moment and dozes off again.
- **Good Game is remade from Roblox's own clap and wave** (Alan, to the plan in words: "yes, build it"): clap, wave,
  stand and breathe, round and round; the nod is dropped (Roblox has none, and a typed-in one is what he rejected).
- **Emotes show feelings through movement** (Alan: "we need to portray emotions through their movements because we
  cannot control their face"). Good Game is a runner-up's grudging one: a slow clap and a slow, unenthusiastic wave.
- **Good Game as approved:** three slow claps a second apart in one smooth rhythm ("1, 2, 3 ... never waiting"),
  the hands meeting; the arms drop; the right arm up into a low V, elbow down, hand beside the head ("if the hand
  comes down to the chin, that's fine"), five lazy waves 0.8 s apart; the weight on one leg throughout. No nod.
- **Nap, Alan's own version** (his "yes, build it"): the napper is bored and done. "Sitting crisscross applesauce, elbow on
  the knee, holding their head up", asleep, a snore bubble at the mouth ("it pops when they jolt awake, like a cartoon")
  and three Z's on the other side; at 1st's light they jolt up, "look around like side to side. 1, 2, 3. Then they look
  forward", and fall back asleep onto the hand with "the touch and go of falling back asleep". This replaces "hands in
  the lap, chin sunk on the chest". (He first asked for legs dangling off the books, then chose this.) His other
  ending, the arm and then the head falling, was left out on my advice: too long beside Camera Flick.
- **Nap after his first look:** sitting emotes always rest on the books ("we need a way to connect the base of the
  model to the top of the book"); the sleeping head hangs limp, face hidden ("it makes them look awake, if you can
  see their faces"); the elbow anywhere on that leg, the hand anywhere on the head.
- **Next One, plan in words: "yes, build it"** (the feeling: already over it and planning the rematch; head down on
  the work, brisk exact folds, the plane held up and turned over; the paper folds as hinged pieces and the hand
  rides the flap). No sighting along the plane towards 1st at the end ("leave the sighting out"). Built the same day; for him to reverse: the sheet is 1 stud long, not a forearm and hand (an avatar's arms cannot reach across a bigger one), and it is held across the body with its nose to the kid's left. With it his go for one Play run of mine to measure Nap's arm glitch.
- **Next One after his first look:** every run of the hand over the paper changes it once: a run and it is flat, a
  run and a fold, a run and another fold, a run and it is done, then they pick it up. No hand moves in between.
- His go for the nine generator tries (each uploads a private animation to his account) and for Edit-mode screen
  grabs.
- Earlier the same day: the winners' shot scales to the screen (as close as the screen allows, across and up and
  down); "ok its good". Details on the [modes and scoring](../systems/modes-and-scoring.md) page.

## Why
Runner-ups get something of their own to do that stays small beside 1st's emote. Sitting already in the dark avoids
a standing shape dropping into a sit at the click.
