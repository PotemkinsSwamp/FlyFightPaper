# Magician: Roblox's Godlike emote for the float, planes from above, a bow, the planes scatter

**Decided:** 2026-11-22 (Alan, in two answers; his words are in the [emote log](../workshop/emote-making.md))
**Status:** OK'd in words ("Well, that works"); nothing built.

## Decision
- **The body is Roblox's own "Godlike" emote** ("Yeah, I'm talking about the godlike emote"): the kid stands, goes into its pose and floats up. No whistle (Claude's first card had one; he replaced it).
- **The paper planes come from above** and fly around the kid once they are up.
- **Coming back down, the kid sweeps one arm into a bow** (from Claude's first card; he asked for it back).
- **At that moment the planes scatter outwards all at once, one passing close by the lens** (the first card's ending; "Well, that works").
- **Not spoken to, Claude's defaults:** the emote at seven tenths of its speed, the scene lifting the kid to about half their height, plain white folded paper planes, six of them, no hit on the lens.

## Why
- The floating emote already exists and already looks right: use Roblox's instead of making one.
- He keeps the bow and the scatter so the trick has an ending.
