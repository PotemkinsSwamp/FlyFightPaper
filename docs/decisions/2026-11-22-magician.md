# Magician: Roblox's Godlike emote for the float, planes from above, a curtsy, the planes leave in a line over the camera and shake it

**Decided:** 2026-11-22 (Alan, in two answers; his words are in the [emote log](../workshop/emote-making.md))
**Status:** OK'd in words ("Well, that works"); built 2026-11-22 ([how](../workshop/emote-making.md)), not yet run in Play or seen by Alan.

## Decision
- **The body is Roblox's own "Godlike" emote** ("Yeah, I'm talking about the godlike emote"): the kid stands, goes into its pose and floats up. No whistle (Claude's first card had one; he replaced it).
- **The paper planes come from above** and fly around the kid once they are up.
- **Coming back down, the kid sweeps one arm into a bow** (from Claude's first card; he asked for it back).
- **At that moment the planes scatter outwards all at once, one passing close by the lens** (the first card's ending; "Well, that works"). **Changed by him on the first recording:** "I think it would be cooler if they followed in a line and flew past the camera but otherwise good stuff": no scatter; the planes leave one behind the other and the line flies past the camera ([his notes](../playtests/2026-11-22-magician-first-run.md)). **And on the second recording:** "Wouldn't it be cooler if they flew over the camera becoming large on screen and shaking the camera slightly like if an actual plane flew over it would cause a lot of vibration"; the plan for it OK'd ("Ok"): the line passes just over the lens, a little right of the middle, each plane about as wide as the picture for a moment, and the camera trembles slightly as each goes over ([his notes](../playtests/2026-11-22-magician-second-run.md)). Not built.
- **The bow is Roblox's own "Curtsy" emote, played over Godlike** (Claude's pick: the only bow-like emote Roblox makes). **Seen in the first recording: it is a curtsy, not a bow** (no forward tip, no arm sweep); Alan: **"Keep the curtsy"**: it stays as the ending; no bow clip is made.
- **Not spoken to, Claude's defaults:** the emote at seven tenths of its speed, the scene lifting the kid to about half their height, plain white folded paper planes, six of them, no hit on the lens.

## Why
- The floating emote already exists and already looks right: use Roblox's instead of making one.
- He keeps the bow and the scatter so the trick has an ending.
