# Hangar: cards are tried on first, Select puts them on, and the exact numbers show

**Date:** 2026-10-21. **Decided by:** Alan, after playing the Hangar Tour.

## What
- The stat sheet shows the real number after each row's boxes ("Health 126", "Lift 93s", "Weight 2/6") on both
  Hangars (match and Range). Alan: the boxes alone "lost more accurate configuration".
- In Customize, clicking a card only **tries it on**: the plane shows it, each number shows its change from what
  you have on now (green better, red worse), and a **Select** button puts it on. What you selected is then what the
  next card is compared with. Cancel, or clicking the card you have on, stops trying. A part you don't own shows Buy
  instead of Select.
- Gear comes off with Clear spot (clicking gear already on a spot used to take it off; it now just stops trying).

## Why
Alan: comparing against the saved build was confusing once a new part was on ("why is it still comparing to the
Classic Fold?"); he wants to compare each card against what he has selected, then choose.
