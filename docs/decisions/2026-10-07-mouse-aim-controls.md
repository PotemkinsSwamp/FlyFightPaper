# Mouse-aim flying (War Thunder style)

**Decided:** 2026-10-07 (Alan, D1)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
A free aim circle follows the mouse; the plane flies itself toward it and banks into turns.

## Why
The old steering was a rate joystick, which gave wide, sloppy turns and failed Alan's playtest.
