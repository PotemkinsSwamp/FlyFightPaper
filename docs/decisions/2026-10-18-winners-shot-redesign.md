# Winners' shot: one steady group shot, like the big games

**Decided:** 2026-10-18 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force; not built yet

## Decision
- **Reference:** Alan's clips of winner screens (Black Ops 3 "Winners Circle", Street Fighter characters in Apex
  Legends, a third shooter's winners screen). What they share: the top 3 stand side by side, big (head to knees
  fill most of the screen) on a plain background; all three emote **at the same time** (1st in the middle, a little
  forward); a calm camera; a name plate under each with place and score; sparks or confetti; about 10 seconds.
- **Stage:** keep the desks. The top 3 stand on three neighbouring chalkboard-row desks, side by side.
- **Camera:** one steady eye-level group shot of all three emoting at once (maybe a slow push in), name plates with
  place and score, confetti. Replaces the current one-at-a-time close-ups and long glides.
- **Emotes:** Roblox's own emotes for now (smooth professional dances and cheers, free); test which ones load in our
  game. The Meshy plane emote waits for the shop stage (Alan: "better but it does look odd").
- **Bot looks and names** (same day): bots wear outfits real players saved, including non-human ones (mice, bears),
  as real players do; names in today's styles.

## Why
Alan: the current cutscene doesn't look like what players know; the reference games focus in on the three winners,
everything moves naturally, and the design is clean. That is what to copy.
