# Camera Flick's move, the plane in the hand, and winners as real models

**Decided:** 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20 (Alan)  
**Status:** built behind the Studio Dev button "end: flick"; the winners' own lamps light up late (not fixed); the
animation is not uploaded

## Decision
- **Camera Flick is approved** from its preview ("this is what I imagined"), facing the camera for the throw.
- **Something happens before the throw, and it is slow.** 1st starts with the elbow tucked into the side, the hand
  and plane up in front of the chest, looking only slightly down at the plane's bottom; the elbow goes out and then
  forward while the forearm rolls over, so they look at its top; that is held; they look at the camera, wind back and
  throw. "It needs to be kind of slow and unrushed, because that's the whole point. It's smug."
- **The throw is a paper-plane throw,** held and pushed forward, not a baseball throw.
- **The plane sits snugly in the hand on every body, with nothing tuned per body.** The hand's own turn need not look
  right. Built as Roblox's own tool hold: fixed to each avatar's grip point.
- **The winners are real models, not layers.** Drawn in overlay layers they came out pixelated; they now stand in the
  room as real models under real spotlights.
- **No confetti** in the winners' moment. **No zoom in** on 1st; the camera stays on all three.
- **The book podiums** are single books stacked by us (one can be kicked later), 1st on six, 2nd on four, 3rd on two.
- **No video or photos of Alan** for an AI or for Roblox. A pose that words cannot carry is settled with numbered
  stills of the real model for him to point at.

Alan played each cut in Studio and said what was wrong in his own words; the notes are in the
