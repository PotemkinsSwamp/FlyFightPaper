# The emote shop: one menu to buy and equip, your character on a box TV

**Decided:** 2026-11-24 (Alan, talking the shop through)
**Status:** How it works approved; first build seen by him 2026-11-25 ([his look](../playtests/2026-11-25-emotes-page-first-look.md): the ranking given, "30,000 papers is too much", the door moved to the main menu, the page needs drawn pictures). The whole note:
[emote shop plan](../plan/EMOTE-SHOP-PLAN.md).

## Decision
- **The shop is also where you equip** ("Is the emote menu also the shop? ... I don't see why it can't be dual
  purpose").
- **You see your own character do the emote** in a small window ("regardless, you need to be able to see your
  character doing the emote"), just your character, in front of a neutral school background.
- **The window is a box TV on a rolling cart**, late 90s / early 2000s ("they would roll in a big box TV on a cart").
  "The cart needs to be very subtle. That's really the TV that's the main picture." His four photos are in
  `.planning/emote-shop/`.
- **A tap flicks the TV on** ("whenever you click one of the emotes, it like flickers on. In the way a box TV used to
  come on, and then it plays the video of your character doing the emote, and then it stops"). The same card again
  replays it; another card starts that one.
- **Two kinds, by these names:** "leader emote and runner up emote".
- **The cards scroll; the TV and the buy button stay** ("it'll scroll down, leaving the TV where it's at with the buy
  paper"). Cards show the signature pose.
- **Two buy buttons, Paper and Robux** ("it should have 2 buttons, one with paper, one with Robux").
- **Prices like skins, the best ones dear** ("Our prices need to be comparable to the value of a skin"; "some of our
  best ones should be like really expensive, so like it encourages people to pay Robux for them"). He will say which
  emotes are the cooler ones ("I'll go through and I'll say which ones ... and we'll price them that way").
- **Prices, 2026-11-25** ("this looks good to me"): Leader 599 / 449 / 299 R$ and Runner-up 249 / 149 R$, each
  Paper price its Robux price x 20; the camera emotes are the coolest. Table and what other games charge:
  [plan](../plan/EMOTE-SHOP-PLAN.md#prices).
- **The door is on the main menu, under Shop and Missions** ("the emotes shouldn't be in the shop"), 2026-11-25.
- **The page gets a new classroom background, the TV rolled into it** ("dont resue the menu background ... a
  background that looks like a classroom like the reference images i gave you"), drawn by Codex ("go for codex"),
  2026-11-25.
- **The classroom picture is approved as drawn** (2026-11-25, from the assembled picture: "this looks great to me";
  the cart: "it looks good. If eventually we have to change it because we'll add more emotes, we can change it then").
  Nine card spots a board, the black TV on its cabinet cart: [the picture](../plan/EMOTE-SHOP-PLAN.md#the-pages-picture-2026-11-25).
- **What plays inside the TV must look as good as the page** (same message): "the character doing the emote ... needs
  to look good too. So there needs to be a nice background, lighting, textures as well."
- The other game's videos are not needed ("We don't have to worry about the other game").
- **The TV's sound is "sound 3"** (2026-11-25, by ear of three): a knob click, static on switching on. **A sound plays
  once however fast you click** ("The sound should only play one time, no matter how fast you click it").
- **Cards: every kid faces you, besides Sore Loser, and fills the same share of the picture** (2026-11-25: "if
  they're like leaning over or they're crouched, it should be the same size").
- **A sitting kid sits on their bottom** ("the character's bottom is supposed to be touching the book, not their
  shoes", 2026-11-25), and his "yes" the same night to a new sitting pose for Nap, then the same fix for the others.
- **Emotes are done for now** (2026-11-25, after close-ups of the five sitting emotes on his avatar: "those are fine
  lets move on from emotes now"). Parked, not dropped: a better picture inside the TV (the line above) and quieter
  emote sounds in the page.
