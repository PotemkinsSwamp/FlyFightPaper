# Aiming lesson in slow motion; Combat Training Pals can't fold you

**Decided:** 2026-10-18/19 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
- **A separate Aiming lesson** between Flight School and Combat Training: the aim circle vs the nose dot, the green
  dot, the orange lead ring, and its own part for the eraser's lock-on. "Just telling them to shoot is not going to be
  enough."
- **Aiming runs in slow motion** ("like we're slowing time down ... then speeding it back up"): the plane and targets
  slow down, then a full-speed part at the end.
- **Combat Training:** the Pal that shows Health going down only shoots until it's lowered, then stops; the eraser Pal's
  eraser is slow enough to dodge; a crash has a smooth way back (countdown, auto take-off, same step).
- **Music:** Liberty Bell for the tutorial's combat lessons; Sprint, Comedy Clip (a), Tough Guys (A) and Silly Chase
  join the match playlist.
- Training Room time goes to 10 minutes (Claude's suggestion, OK'd with the plan).

## Why
New players don't know the aiming system, and learning it at full speed while flying forward is too hard (Alan).
Dying to a teaching enemy would feel unfair and stop the lesson.
