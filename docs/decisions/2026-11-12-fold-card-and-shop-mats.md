# Fold card in the Hangar, and shop picture backgrounds

**Decided:** 2026-11-12 (Alan, playtest of the money plan)
**Status:** decided and built; not yet seen in Play.

## Decision
- **Folded by a part you don't own: a card, not a line on the top note.** It shows the part itself (no stats), whose it
  was, the level it unlocks at and its Paper price. Paper first: "Buy" with Paper when you have the level and the
  Paper; otherwise what you're waiting for ("Wait for level N" / "Need N more"). Always a Buy now for Robux button.
- It starts on the fold screen and carries into the Hangar; it goes away with its X, LAUNCH, or going elsewhere.
- **Once per part per play session.**
- **Shop pictures sit on faded chalk green** (RGB 104,133,118), Alan's pick from real-part mock-ups
  ([design examples](../workshop/design-examples.md)): middle brightness, so white and dark pieces both show; brown
  kraft ("looked very bad") and midnight indigo (dark pieces sank) were rejected. Reopened the same day: in Play it
  "just doesn't look right" (it came out olive); from three fixes he picked **mint green paper** (tint 96,170,153;
  Today's parts circles a little smaller, clear of the price label)
  ([fourth study](../workshop/design-examples.md#why-the-green-looks-wrong-in-play-fourth-study-2026-11-12)).
- **Fold card: no circle, a soft shadow** under the turning part (picked over an indigo circle).
- **Today's parts timer:** one line ("New parts in 1h 44m") on dark paper between the heading and the cards, counted
  on the server's clock so it ends at the same moment for everyone.

## Why
Alan: the line on the top note was in a place that didn't drive the point home; the card shows what got you and how to
get it, when you're looking. Paper on white paper had no contrast.
