# Chalk eraser rulings after playtest

**Decided:** 2026-10-14/15 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
The eraser is half its first size. Its better model is built in Blender from the approved model sheet
(`assets/models/toys/chalk-eraser/model-sheet.png`), never from artistic concepts. The cloud is 2.25x bigger with a
thick dust look (no cartoon balls). Planes inside are hard to see, not invisible. Name tags and targeting are gone
only for planes inside the cloud. Second playtest (same day): cloud another 30% bigger; inside it you still see the
world, just hazy; cooldown 3 min -> 2 min; ready sign = the felt is clean and dark after a puff and chalk slowly comes
back (lots of white, black still showing, "still needs to look like an eraser"). Third playtest: inside should be like
fog (see only so far ahead), cloud lasts 30 s instead of 20, and it shrinks as it thins out, protecting you until it
is small enough to vanish. Fourth: 45 s, cloud 20% smaller, see a bit further in the fog, and the cloud blocks
targeting and outlines like a building, also between two planes outside it on opposite sides.

## Why
Alan's playtest: the first cloud was too small, and the eraser was too big for a dropped classroom eraser.
