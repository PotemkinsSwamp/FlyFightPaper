# Plane colours by mode

**Decided:** 2026-10-13 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
Team deathmatch: blue team planes are blue with circles, red team red with squares. Free-for-all: every plane is split down the middle, left half red with a square, right half blue with a circle, and the split includes the body, nose and tail.

## Why
Plain white faded into the cream room; thin wings alone showed almost no colour.
