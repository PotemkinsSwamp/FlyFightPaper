# Modes workshop: moving zone, team colours, paper targets

**Decided:** 2026-10-13 (Alan, D13)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
The Airspace Control zone is a field big enough to fly circles in (start about 400 studs across and 200 tall; tune in playtest), and it moves every 90 s among about 5 built-up spots (15 s warning: next spot outlined plus an edge arrow), one of them wide and flat up in the ceiling decorations. Team deathmatch gets team colours back. Breakable props become hidden kid-drawn paper targets (things kids dislike) on buildings: no hints, per player, fall flat when hit, stay down for the round, server-checked. Fan Alley is dropped (power-ups cover it) in favour of fly-through obstacles that help lose a chaser, e.g. a fan you time through. Practice mode lives in the tutorial (D12).

## Why
A small static zone was a prop, not a fight; targets give a reason to explore the town.
