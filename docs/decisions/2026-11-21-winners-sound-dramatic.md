# The winners' moment sounds dramatic, with one background for every emote

**Decided:** 2026-11-21 (Alan)
**Status:** decided in words; nothing built or picked yet. What the background sounds like is still open.

## Decision
- **The celebratory sounds go** (the crowd's cheering and clapping, the kids' "Yeah!", the winners' songs under the
  emotes): "we need to go away with the celebratory sounds, and it needs to be a more dramatic thing". This replaces
  the mix of 2026-10-23 (`src/client/WinnersSound.luau`).
- **Still sound effects, with faint background music under them**: "if it's just sound effects, it'll be very odd".
- **The shape** (his "that's what I see as well"): the dark nearly silent; a switch sound for each of the three
  lights; the emotes' own sounds over a faint background; an end.
- **Nothing in the scene's sound hangs on one emote** (his correction: I had Camera Flick's hit on the lens end the
  music): "we will be introducing other emotes, so it can't be tied too much to that one". The background and the
  end are the same whatever 1st plays.
- **One universal background, no song per emote for now**: "we can add the songs to emotes as something later if we
  decide to". The [winners' circle plan](../plan/WINNERS-CIRCLE-PLAN.md) lists a song in every emote's recipe; until
  he decides otherwise an emote brings sound effects only. Nothing is built ahead for a song of its own.

## Why
The approved emotes are attitude and rivalry in the dark under spotlights (a slow clap, a nap, the next plane, a
plane flicked at the camera); a party mix fights that. One background means every new emote works at once and the
free emote most players see sounds as finished as a paid one.
