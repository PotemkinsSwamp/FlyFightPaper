# Screen fit: every piece in its place on any screen

**Decided:** 2026-10-24 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
- The [screen fit plan](../plan/SCREEN-FIT-PLAN.md) is approved: one size rule (drawn size x screen scale, kept
  between a minimum and a maximum), a screen map where every piece over the game has its own area, the same
  minimums on every menu word and button, and an automatic overlap check on every device.
- Pieces may move a little on a PC too, shown to Alan as before/after pictures.
- The kill feed shows 2 short lines on a phone.
- Alan switches the Device Simulator's device when asked (Claude doesn't click in Studio).

## Why
On a phone the Menu button, the match strip and the kill feed piled up; Alan: "every UI element ... needs to follow
this minimum maximum size and where it belongs, so it always looks the way we want it to on any screen."
