# Desk quick menu: pieces round the edges (concept B)

**Decided:** 2026-10-22 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
- The in-match desk menu follows `assets/references/ui-concepts/desk-quick-menu-b.png` (picked over the single cork
  board of `-a`): pieces pinned round the screen edges in the new Hangar's art, the middle left open.
- It must not cover what the match already shows: the match strip (score and clock, top middle) and the kill feed
  (top right) stay in view at the desk, so the concept's own score note is dropped.
- The short stat sheet has four rows: **Health, Speed, Lift, Boost** (no Climb, Swerve or Weight; Customize has those).
- **Customize** opens the Hangar's binder (the "book") straight away.
- Claude's calls (for Alan to reverse): the level bar is left out; Boost's row is teal so it doesn't match Speed's
  blue; the builds' small photos have no cork strip behind them yet.

## Why
Alan: B lets the match stay visible (strip and kill feed) while everything the desk needs is still there.
