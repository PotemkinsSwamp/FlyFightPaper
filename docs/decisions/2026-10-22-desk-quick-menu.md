# Desk quick menu: pieces round the edges (concept B)

**Decided:** 2026-10-22 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
- The in-match desk menu follows `assets/references/ui-concepts/desk-quick-menu-b.png` (picked over the single cork
  board of `-a`): pieces pinned round the screen edges in the new Hangar's art, the middle left open.
- It must not cover what the match already shows: the match strip (score and clock, top middle) and the kill feed
  (top right) stay in view at the desk, so the concept's own score note is dropped.
- The short stat sheet has four rows: **Health, Speed, Lift, Boost** (no Climb, Swerve or Weight; Customize has those).
- **Customize** opens the Hangar's binder (the "book") straight away.
- After his first look (same day, from an assembled preview before upload): the left side sits level with the match
  strip (the big photo starts just under Roblox's buttons and the Menu button, which hold that corner); the build photos
  are real taped photos, each turned a little, on a frameless cork strip; the stat paper is bigger and its rows keep
  clear of the binder holes; LAUNCH! is much bigger on bright green paper; the right-hand strips go big, middle, small,
  middle, big (Refill and Customize biggest, Fresh plane smallest) and fill the side; strips carry no drawn pins (a pin
  must never stretch with its paper) and are pinned at both top corners with the kill feed's own pins at its size
  (tape was tried and "takes too much attention").
- Claude's calls (for Alan to reverse): the level bar is left out; Boost's row is teal so it doesn't match Speed's
  blue; the red strip's pins are blue so they show.

- **Approved by Alan in a match, same day:** "The quick menu looks good. We can move on."

## Why
Alan: B lets the match stay visible (strip and kill feed) while everything the desk needs is still there.
