# Art target is the approved reference picture

**Decided:** 2026-10-01 (Alan), sharpened 2026-10-09  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
Every building, prop and room finish is styled on `assets/references/classroom-city-approved-v1.png`. Never style new art on the buildings already in the game.

## Why
The in-game buildings are rough, low-quality versions of that picture. Matching them would make new art low quality too (Alan, 2026-10-09).
