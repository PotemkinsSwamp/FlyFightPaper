# What bots had to do first

**Decided:** 2026-10-07 (Alan, D7)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
Bots play free-for-all and team deathmatch first (the zone waited for its design). A Studio spectator camera follows any bot and shows what it is trying to do. Build order: navigation, brain, skill/personality, looks.

## Why
Get bots fighting well in the simple modes before adding objectives.
