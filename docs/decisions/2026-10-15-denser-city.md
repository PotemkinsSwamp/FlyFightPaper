# A denser city, still open to fly through

**Decided:** 2026-10-15 (Alan)
**Status:** in force

## Decision
- Make the city denser, like the reference picture: bustling and full, but not packed. You must still be able to fly
  between buildings, up and down, freely.
- Right now there is a lot of empty space. Fill it, including the floor outside the rug play area.
- Done as a **full replan of the rug and the town together**, not by dropping buildings into gaps: buildings never
  on roads, never too close, laid out consistently (Alan, after seeing 21 copies patched into the gaps; they were
  removed). A top-down preview picture gets his OK before anything is built.

## Why
The reference picture reads as a busy toy city; the game's town has big empty gaps that make it feel sparse.
