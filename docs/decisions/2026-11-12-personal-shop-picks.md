# Personal shop picks: A, B, F, G, H, J, N, P; offers after a fold or loss when they fit; funnel the buying

**Decided:** 2026-11-12 (Alan, after the [personal shop research](../research/PERSONAL-SHOP-RESEARCH-2026-11-12.md))
**Status:** decided; nothing built. Replaces the "never after a loss" rule in the
[shop direction](2026-11-11-shop-direction.md) and the [shop research](../research/SHOP-RESEARCH-2026-11-11.md).

## Decision
- **Yes:** A (starter pack for players who haven't bought yet), B (double Paper on the first buy of each Paper pack),
  F (your next part on the Featured card), G (the right Paper pack on a failed buy), H (Today's parts, changing daily),
  J (free daily gift), N (a no-price line on the fold screen naming the weapon that folded you), P (no more offers
  after 3 buys in a day). Their numbers still come to Alan for his OK before building.
- **Offers after a fold or a lost match: allowed when they fit.** Not selling an undo (there's nothing to undo), but
  the thing that helps at that moment: "want to earn more Paper / XP?" (the 2x boosts). Only where it fits the moment.
- **The shop should funnel players into spending** (Alan: "these are marketing strategies"): decoy packs, a ladder
  of offers and the like, using normal marketing tricks. It's fair because nothing is sold that can't be bought by
  everyone, at a price shown up front.
- **EU rules: not now.** No real-money value next to Paper (no other game does it). If the EU proposal becomes law
  (2028 at the earliest), look at how other Roblox developers handle it, then maybe an EU-only version of the menu.
- Roblox's own rules still apply (they're the platform's, not the EU's): real timers, real sales, Roblox's purchase
  confirm on every buy.

## Why
Alan wants the shop to make money; the research's safest options were too timid for that. Offers after a fold
are about the reward a player is chasing, not their loss.
