# The playtime chart takes 2 hours, not 4

**Decided:** 2026-11-25 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force; replaces the "chest at 4 hours" in [real prices only](2026-11-25-real-prices-only.md)

## Decision
Alan: "I think 4 hours is a bit excessive. I think maybe we drop it down to 2. We just fit everything back into 2
hours." The chart keeps all 16 boxes and every prize, one box every 7½ minutes (`playtime.box_seconds` 450 in
`config/economy.json`): big boxes at 30 minutes, 1 hour and 1 hour 30, the treasure chest at 2 hours. Same day: "I kind
of like the idea of the treasure map" for the look (pictures to come).

## Why
Four hours is a very long day for a kid. A box every 7½ minutes is about one a match, and the median player (about
17 minutes a day) now reaches two boxes instead of one. The chart now gives its whole worth in half the time: a
2-hour player gets about 80% more Paper than playing alone, chest included (numbers on the plan).
