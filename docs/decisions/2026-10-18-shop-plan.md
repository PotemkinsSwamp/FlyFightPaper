# What the shop sells, and the plane retexture comes first

**Decided:** 2026-10-18 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force; prices drafted ([pricing research](../research/PRICING-RESEARCH-2026-10-18.md)), wait on Alan

## Decision
- **Order:** retexture and re-detail every plane part first (paper texture, creases, shading, cleaner look; one
  sample part for Alan's OK, then the rest), then pricing research, then the shop (switched off until Alan approves
  prices), then the tutorial and Stage 11.
- **Sold:** the boosts and Paper already in [VISION](../vision/VISION.md); **early part unlocks** (one part, fixed
  Robux price set by its level, the same for everyone; never XP or levels); **emotes** (cheap for free-asset ones,
  more for custom ones); **trails**; **stickers**; **fold effects** (what an enemy's plane does when you take it
  down; no gore); **paper types** (graph paper, newspaper, foil...); **VIP pass**; **private servers** with match
  settings (1v1s, bot difficulty, like Call of Duty's custom games).
- **No "unlock every part" buy** (it would make levels and the XP pass pointless); instead body bundles: one body
  with its matching parts, priced high.
- **Permanent boosts cost a lot** because they last forever (2x Paper about $19, 2x XP less, since levels have a cap).
- **Cosmetic prices slide** with how detailed and how wanted an item is: simple ones cost cents, detailed popular
  ones a few dollars.
- **Plane skins are whole-plane looks:** you still pick parts for their stats; the skin draws over the whole plane
  and stretches to the plane's real size (a small fast build shows it smaller, a bulky build bigger), so what you
  see always matches what can be hit.
- **Sticker editor:** turn your plane around in the hangar and stick stickers anywhere (sticker-bomb it or place a
  few with care).
- **Teams stay readable** through every skin and sticker (team outlines, names and colours stay on top).
- **Special planes for Paper:** ready-made planes with their own look (our own designs, e.g. a paper flapping-wing
  craft; nothing copied from films). Fixed builds, no customising. Priced high in Paper so you save up or buy Paper
  packs (already allowed in VISION). Any special weapon on one can also be bought for your own planes.
- **Draw on your plane** (Alan: freehand, visible to everybody), sold as a pass. Safeguards: a report button on any
  plane (a reported drawing hides for the reporter at once, after a few reports for everyone); an outside check
  that runs away from the game (saved drawings read through Roblox Open Cloud, judged by an AI, a flag written
  back), so the game itself makes no AI calls; a flagged drawing is removed and the player asked to redraw, never
  banned on one flag; losing drawing for good only after repeat flags that Alan has looked at, because they paid.
  The pass's terms say before purchase that drawing offensive things can lose the drawing ability. The outside
  check costs money per drawing and needs a machine running it: Alan's go before it is built.
- **Prestige (rebirth):** reset your level for prestige-only skins and trails; it also makes 2x XP worth more.
  To design before the shop.
- **Later:** a desk decorating system (stand at your desk and arrange decorations), the season sticker book.

## Why
Alan, 2026-10-18: the game should make money with things players want to show off. Bland plane parts would make
every skin look worse, so they get better first. Skins that cover the whole plane solve "how does a skin look
right when parts swap". Freehand drawing seen by others isn't checked by Roblox's text filters, and this account has
already been banned twice over one texture that looked offensive by accident, so drawing needs its own safeguards.
