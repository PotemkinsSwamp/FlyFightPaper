# Escapes: ceiling ducts and the chalk-eraser cloud

**Decided:** 2026-10-14 (Alan, D14)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
HVAC ducts above a drop ceiling: a big loop round the room plus a cross through the middle, about 6 vents, 10-15 s to cross. Inside: 2-3 spinning fans to time through, a main blower at the middle, one narrow branch heavy planes can't fit, dim light. Only veteran bots use them. Plus a dropped chalk eraser in the town: shoot it for a big chalk cloud (about 250 studs, 20 s) that hides planes, names and lock-ons; back after 3 min. No points for shaking someone off. Concepts approved 2026-10-14.

## Why
Open-air obstacles don't lose a chaser; they just fly around them.
