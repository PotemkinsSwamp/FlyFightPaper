# Bot planes are balanced on purpose, never random

**Decided:** 2026-10-07 (Alan, D8)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
Measure every weapon and part (damage per second, time to kill, how hard to dodge) and define beginner, balanced and challenging builds. Rookies get forgiving builds; experienced bots get harder-to-dodge, higher-damage, faster-firing weapons and sharper planes. Alan approves the tier table before bots use it.

## Why
Random builds make some bots unfair and others useless.
