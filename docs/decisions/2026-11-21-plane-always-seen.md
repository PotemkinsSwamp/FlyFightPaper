# A held plane is always seen, on every body

**Decided:** 2026-11-21 (Alan: "go on the recommendation")
**Status:** built for the plane emotes that fly the plane with the hand (Toy Pilot and the standing 1st-place plane emote); checked by numbers in Edit only, not yet seen in Play by anyone. Camera Flick is approved and untouched.

## Why
Alan played Toy Pilot's fourth version as 1st on his own avatar, a stout little dog in a superhero costume: "you couldn't see the paper airplane because the model was too wide ... you could barely see any of the emote because the arm is so small ... I'm worried about character models and how this can work".

Measured in Edit on his account's avatar against a plain blocky kid:

| | Plain kid | His avatar |
|---|---|---|
| Body height | 5.5 studs | 3.5 studs |
| Arm, shoulder to grip | 2.0 studs | 0.65 studs |
| Hand out sideways, past the body's edge | 2.0 studs | 0.3 studs |
| Hand straight up | 0.6 studs above the head | stops half-way up the head |

The shoulder sits inside the head's width and the plane (1.5 studs) is more than twice the arm: no arm pose carries it clear of that body. Many players wear small animal bodies like it.

## Decision
- **The plane must always be seen; the hand holds it when it can.** Seen from the camera, a plane whose middle sits over the body or over what is worn on the head is slid outward to the edge. On a body whose hand already holds it clear, nothing moves. On a short-armed body the plane glides just beyond the hand and the arm and head follow it: a kid whose paper plane flies round them. The rule and its numbers are on the [modes and scoring](../systems/modes-and-scoring.md) page.
- **The catch he was told:** on short-armed bodies there is a gap between hand and plane. Whether that reads as charming or wrong is his call in Play.

## Passed over
- **A standard kid body on the podium** (own head, hat and colours, normal arms): every emote would work, but his dog stops being a dog at its winning moment.
- **A bigger plane on small bodies:** its tail would still be inside the head.

## Still open
- The runner-up emotes (Good Game, Nap, Next One) have the same short-arm limit; approved and not measured on his avatar. He was asked whether he wants them measured.
