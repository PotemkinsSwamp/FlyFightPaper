# Gift a friend: Paper packs and timed 2x boosts, to friends who have played

**Decided:** 2026-11-13 (Alan, after the [gift plan](../plan/GIFT-PLAN.md) walkthrough: "take everything I said. Go
ahead and build.")
**Status:** decided; being built (Studio only until go-live).

## Decision
- **What can be gifted: Paper packs and the timed 2x boosts.** Not the forever passes ("just to make it easier...
  we can add it later if gifts sell"), not the starter pack ("that wouldn't even make sense"), not the Paper jar, not
  Double this match.
- **The Gift a friend button at the top of the shop stays**, even though every card has its own gift button (Alan:
  "kind of redundant... we can keep it there").
- **Friends only**, never strangers, never yourself. The friend must have played at least once ("Invite them
  first!", nothing charged).
- **No "First buy 2x!" on gifts, and a gift never uses up the giver's own first-buy 2x** (Alan: "if the first thing
  you buy is a gift, it doesn't ruin your first buy").
- **Gifted Paper never fills the jar.** Nothing new: only Paper earned in matches fills it
  ([jar rules](2026-11-12-paper-jar-rules.md)).
- **A gift counts as one of the giver's 3 buys a day** (the calm rule: after 3, offer pop-ups stop).
- **At most 10 gifts wait for one friend.** A full friend shows as unable to get a gift before anything is paid
  ("Sam has 10 gifts waiting!").
- **No "ask a friend to buy me this" button.**
- **The friend note has a search box** (Alan 2026-11-13, on seeing it): type part of a friend's name.
- **The gift arrives by paper plane** (Alan's pick of three looks, 2026-11-13): over the main menu a plane swoops in
  carrying the wrapped box, drops it, the box bursts open and a sticky note says who sent what, with Thanks!.

## Why
Gifts are a second reason to buy without pushing anyone: the giver chooses, the friend never asks. Packs and boosts
only add Paper or boost time, so little can go wrong; the passes are Roblox game passes tied to their buyer and would
need a copy kept in our save, the riskiest part for the fewest sales.
