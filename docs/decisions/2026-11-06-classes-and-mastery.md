# Six classes, four new weapons and mastery

**Decided:** 2026-11-06 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force; details and tuning on the [classes plan](../plan/CLASSES-PLAN.md)

## Decision
- Six fixed class planes that keep unlocking up to about level 10, with low-tier weapons only.
- Hole Punch, Stubby Crayon, Spitball and Thumbtack join the weapons. No mini eraser. (Names and rules replaced by
  [the 2026-11-07 decision](2026-11-07-four-new-weapons-and-falling-shots.md).)
- Mastery for each class, weapon and part: slow, with a substantial first rank and small steps after it, up to about
  +10% on any one stat. Top ranks need prestige, and mastery stays through prestige. Bots have none.
- Bots follow the lobby's average level, weighted towards its lower-level players.

## Why
One weapon at the start is boring, and the first session has to hook people. Classes show the variety early, levels
still matter because classes can't be changed, and mastery gives a long-term goal. Alan: long-time players must never
feel overpowered to a new player.
