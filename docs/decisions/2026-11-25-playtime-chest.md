# The playtime chart's treasure chest: two sure prizes and a spin wheel

**Decided:** 2026-11-25 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force, not built

## Decision
The last box (2 hours, [decision](2026-11-25-playtime-chart-two-hours.md)) of the [playtime chart](../plan/PLAYTIME-REWARDS-PLAN.md) is a treasure chest that opens into a choice:
3,000 Paper on the left, a spin wheel in the middle, 1 hour of 2x Paper and 1 hour of 2x XP (one prize) on the right.
The wheel: 3,000 Paper 30%, the boost pair 27%, a Sticker chest 25%, 5,000 Paper 10%, a Blue Ribbon chest 6%, a
Golden Star chest 2%. Odds are printed on the wheel.

Alan: "it's still a choice. So you either choose the paper, the 1 hour 2x paper, 1 hour 2xp, or you choose like this
lottery idea"; "in the middle the spin wheel pops up ... to the left of it the 3,000 paper, to the right of it the 1 hour,
2 times paper, 2 times XP"; "let's just remove the paper chest"; "keep it as 1 hour 2 times paper and 1 hour 2 XP
together".

## Why
A kid who wants a sure thing gets one; a kid who wants the thrill spins. The Paper chest (1,000 Paper) was worth too
little next to 3,000 Paper. Boosts stay at an hour so the chart never becomes a free copy of the "2x forever" passes.
Without a low slice the wheel never pays less than a sure prize, so most kids will spin (said to Alan with the numbers).
