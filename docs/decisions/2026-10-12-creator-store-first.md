# Search the free Creator Store first

**Decided:** 2026-10-12 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
Before making any new model, search Roblox's free Creator Store and use a match if it fits the look. Read or delete every script in a free model first.

## Why
A fitting free asset is instant; making every piece costs hours and credits.
