# Bubblegum: sassy all through, a bubble that hides the face, one sheet of gum pulled off the lens

**Decided:** 2026-11-22 (Alan, in four answers; his words are in the [emote log](../workshop/emote-making.md) and the [playtest note](../playtests/2026-11-22-bubblegum-first-run.md))
**Status:** the body is built and he has no complaint about it on the first recording; the bubble's thickness and the sheet of gum are OK'd in words, not built.

## Decision
- **The kid is sassy from start to finish:** weight on one leg, arms hanging loose, head tilted, looking at the camera, and it stays that way. Their cool does not break.
- **The reference video shows the whole act and takes its time:** 15 seconds, with the bubble in it. A 10 second one without the bubble was "a little sped up" and could not be judged.
- **The bubble is nearly solid:** only the kid's shape shows through it, never the face. 2nd and 3rd disappear behind it in its last second; he accepted that.
- **On the lens the gum is one layer:** it sticks, then is pulled off downward as one piece, stretching at the top into strands that snap.
- **The camera does not move in:** the bubble grows to the lens. He has not spoken to this; it follows his [ruling of 2026-11-20](2026-11-20-camera-flick-and-real-winners.md).

## Why
- A face seen through the bubble shows that nobody is blowing it.
- A bunch of lumps does not look like stretched gum; sticky gum comes off in one piece.
- Faces cannot be animated, so the feeling has to stay in the posture the whole time.
