# Menus are made of classroom crafts

**Decided:** 2026-10-08 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
Menus look like an early-2000s classroom, like an I Spy book: crayon and marker lettering, construction paper, tape, push pins, stickers, lined paper. The approved concepts are in `assets/references/ui-concepts/`. The on-screen name is FLY FIGHT PAPER.

## Why
It fits the game's world and stands out from plain Roblox menus.
