# From an approved concept picture to a screen: break it into 2D pieces, assemble in Studio

**Decided:** 2026-11-25 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
Alan: "what you are supposed to do is take a concept image then break it up into 2d assets, if you can use existing
assets in substitution without too much of a noticable difference then you use them if not you make 2d assets out of
everything then assemble them in studio".

1. The approved concept picture is the target. The screen must look like it: same layout, drawings, colours.
2. Break the picture into its 2D pieces (each paper, drawing, button, sticker as its own picture).
3. For each piece: use art the game already has only when the difference would not be noticed; otherwise make the
   piece from the concept, as drawn.
4. Put the pieces together in Studio, at the concept's positions, and compare the result with the concept.

It came from the Treasure Map: a first assembly swapped in loaded shop art, turned the path round and added pieces the
concept did not have. Alan: "that looks a lot different from the concept images so no to that, make it look exactly
like the concept image or dont do it at all."

## Why
The concept is what he approved. Pieces keep the screen flexible (things can move, light up, be counted) while the
look stays the approved one; swapping art is fine only when nobody could tell.
