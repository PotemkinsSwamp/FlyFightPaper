# The beta has to feel like a finished game

**Decided:** 2026-11-06 (Alan)  
**Status:** in force; the systems below are being talked through, none is designed yet

## Decision
- The beta is a finished product with one map. Updates add maps, parts and ideas; they don't finish the game.
- The hook has to be strong in the first session. People who try the game once and aren't hooked won't come back,
  whatever gets added later.
- Real people have tested the game and it plays fine. Larger playtests can come, but they aren't the biggest gap.
  The biggest gap is the features that keep people interested.

## To settle before the beta
- **More weapons and tiers at low levels:** one weapon at the start is boring.
- **Ready-made loadouts by class:** fixed planes such as a fast, fragile striker and a big, slow bomber or carrier,
  with no customising until levels unlock the parts.
- **The drive to level up:** ready-made loadouts mustn't take away the reason to level (Alan's concern).
- **Skins, emotes, finishing the winners' circle, and the real shop.**

## Why
Alan: a beta's first impression decides whether people return, so the beta can't wait on later updates for its hook.
