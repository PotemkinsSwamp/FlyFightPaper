# Bots get human-like quirks

**Decided:** 2026-10-07 (Alan, D9)  
**Status:** in force

## Decision
Each bot gets random quirks: ramming on purpose, a victory loop or taunt after a kill, signature moves. Veterans get more and bolder quirks; some rookies get them too.

## Why
Bots should feel like people, not machines.
