# Ideas not yet planned

Ideas that came up but are not in the [milestones](MILESTONES.md). Nothing here gets built without Alan saying so.
When Alan has a new idea, it goes here first; when he decides on one, it moves into a [decision](../decisions/README.md)
and a stage.

## From Alan
- **Fly-through obstacles that help lose a chaser**, e.g. a fan you have to time your way through (D13; the HVAC
  ducts cover part of this).
- **Which weapon the aim marker follows** when two different weapons are fitted (open question from the
  [2026-10-12 playtest](../playtests/2026-10-12.md)).

- **Personal report card** (Alan 2026-11-11): your own grades per subject (folds, assists, aim, crashes) for
  all-time stats, on its own screen; not for the end of a round ([report card plan](REPORT-CARD-PLAN.md)).
- **Playtime rewards for retention** (Alan 2026-11-12): little rewards for staying and playing, on a growing scale
  (5 minutes, 10, 30, an hour, 2 hours...), shown as a little calendar you fill in. What fills them is open: it waits
  on cosmetics, and small rewards could lead up to them.
- **Cosmetics: skins, trails, emotes** (Alan 2026-11-12: "we have to start looking at" them and the other small
  buys): what to sell and how players earn some of them, maybe a whole system for getting them. Needed before the
  playtime rewards can be filled. Research: [cosmetics research](../research/COSMETICS-RESEARCH-2026-11-12.md).
  Once skins exist, the shop's Today's parts row can rotate skins for players who own every part (Alan 2026-11-12).
- **Redo the part models, boosters first** (Alan 2026-11-12): the booster models don't read as what they do, and
  every part should look more appealing. Revisit all part models later.
- **Bots that use the town** (Alan 2026-11-12): bots never fly through the tubes, rings and holes, never go for
  boosts, and follow one recognisable path. A bot development pass: use fly-throughs, grab boosts, vary routes.
- **Daily and weekly missions** (Alan 2026-11-12): small goals that reset each day and each week, with a reward
  for finishing them. What the goals are and what they pay is open.
- **Skill bonuses** (Alan 2026-11-12): a few extra points for moments that show skill, e.g. folding someone by
  ramming them and surviving it (no bonus if you go down too). Small, named in the score pop-up.
- **Treasure chest for buyers** (Alan 2026-11-13): like the class treasure box at school, but earned by buying:
  after a number of purchases you get to pick something from a chest (e.g. 10,000 Paper), the prizes growing the more
  you've bought, a goal players can see. Not a punch card and never framed as "good" for spending. Check first that
  it's fair to kids and allowed on Roblox (the player picks, so it's not random).

## Later scope (from the vision)
The [vision](../vision/VISION.md) lists what waits until after the first release: cosmetics, more maps, full touch
then controller support, better desk equipment, collapsing cities, capture/payload and escort modes, advanced
ranked systems. Skill-route rewards and pickups are ideas pending design.
