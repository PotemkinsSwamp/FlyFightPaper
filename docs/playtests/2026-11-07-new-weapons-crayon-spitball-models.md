# 2026-11-07: Crayon, Spitball, rams and the four new weapon models

## What Alan did
Played in his open Studio through the evening of 2026-11-07 into 2026-11-08: the Stubby Crayon at each tuning, the
Spitball with the "spitball hunters" dev button (four spitball-only bots), a head-on ram on a bot, then the four new
weapon models and sounds.

## What he saw
- **Crayon:** first version (850 studs/s, 600 range, heavy) far too strong; 400 studs/s, 350 range, drop 30, weight 4
  "good... balanced" at first, then "impossible to hit anybody" after longer play.
- **Spitball:** Lift burn alone never made a chased plane fall in 10-15 s, and the spitter got no assist (1 damage a
  hit). Being hit by four bots felt serious. Asked for a plane to hold 5 in all, for spitballs to be weight (slower,
  sinking, Lift burns faster) without a harsh sink, and for credit by how many of yours are stuck.
- **Bots** flew below top speed; he wants every bot at full top speed, skill only in turning, pitch and brake.
- **Rams:** a bot flew straight through him head-on and nothing happened.
- **Models:** the first concept pictures looked like real photos; he wants game renders. The Blender models' colours
  were weak and too white; the spitball cup needed a holder; the thumbtack's blue base wasn't needed.
- **Sounds:** picked 3, 5, 7, 11 from a list of licensed sounds; "the sounds sound good".
- **End:** "everything is good."

## What it meant
- Crayon now 600 studs/s, 450 range, drop 8 ([decision](../decisions/2026-11-07-four-new-weapons-and-falling-shots.md)).
- Spitballs are weight: 5 per plane in all, 6% slower, 1.5x Lift burn and a gentle 2.5 studs/s sink each; each of
  yours stuck counts as 4 damage toward credit. Alan: "a good support weapon... I'd call this spitball good".
- Bots fly at full top speed ([decision](../decisions/2026-11-07-bots-fly-full-speed.md)).
- Fast head-on passes now count as a ram ([damage and deaths](../systems/damage-and-deaths.md)).
- Concept pictures are game renders ([lessons](../workshop/lessons.md)); the four models were rebuilt in strong colours,
  uploaded (13 pieces, all approved) and given their own sounds ([weapons](../systems/weapons.md)).
