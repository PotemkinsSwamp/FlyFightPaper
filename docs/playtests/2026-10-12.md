# Playtest 2026-10-12: new planes and weapons

Alan's notes after flying the new plane parts and weapons (from that night's session handoff).

1. **Hangar preview fit:** every build fills the same frame, and you can spin it in all directions.
2. **Wings clip into wide bodies:** wings must meet each body's real side.
3. **Binder Gunship must change:** a binder isn't a paper plane. Became the
   [Toilet Roll Gunship](../decisions/2026-10-13-toilet-roll-gunship.md).
4. **Rubber Band Repeater model is a mess:** rebuilt in Blender from plain shapes.
5. **Pencil launcher textures broken:** rebuilt in Blender.
6. **Weapon picking:** clicking a weapon with a mount selected replaces what's there.
7. **Shots look like their weapon:** each weapon fires its own piece (pellet, staple, band, paperclip, eraser, pencil).
8. **Pencil shots a little faster.**
9. **Open question for Alan:** with two different weapons fitted, which one the aim/lead marker follows.
