# Magician: the look approved; Alan's notes on the first sound try

**When:** 2026-11-22. First, on the fly-over build (checked in Edit, never recorded by Claude): "Okay, it's good. Now it just needs sound effects." Then, on `.planning/emotes/magician-sound-try1.mp4`: one Gemini call's sound laid under the **second run's** recording, the one from before the fly-over. How the build and the sound try work: [emote making](../workshop/emote-making.md). Before it: [the second recording](2026-11-22-magician-second-run.md).

## What he said, and what changes
| Alan | What changes |
|---|---|
| "You input the video, the old video. You've since updated. So now it doesn't have the turbulence and the airplanes flying over ... you generated it from a video that is outdated." | Claude's mistake: the sound was made from, and shown under, a recording of the build before the fly-over. The next try starts from a recording of the current build (the planes over the camera, the tremble). A sound is never made from or shown under an outdated recording. |
| "The magical sound, that's too loud. It needs to be something very, very, very, very subtle. Because remember, there's also music going on in the background." | The lift-off tone becomes barely there: it sits under the winners' music. |
| "The whoosh is too loud. The whoosh should only happen when they're flying over the camera, because you're from the camera's perspective, so you should only hear the whoosh as it's flying over the camera. Because that whoosh is something you'd hear if you're like under a plane." | No roar that builds before the planes arrive. A whoosh is heard only at the moment a plane goes over the lens, as from under it, and quieter. |
