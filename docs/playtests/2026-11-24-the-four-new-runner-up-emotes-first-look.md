# Alan's first look at the four new runner-up emotes

**When:** 2026-11-24, Alan in his own Play in Studio, each emote from its Dev button ("2nd/3rd: ..."), built the same day by four side sessions ([the picks](../decisions/2026-11-24-four-more-runner-up-emotes.md)). Nobody had seen them in Play before him.

## What he said, and what changes
| Emote | Alan | What changes | Where it stands |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Sore Loser](../workshop/emotes/sore-loser.md) | "There's not enough body language to really display what's happening ... there's no movement. Like they're not stamping their foot ... the reference video was better at showing it." And: "at the start, turn away from the first place. They need to physically turn and cross their arms and then look back." | The kid starts facing us and, at 1st's light, physically turns away and crosses the arms, on screen; then the look back. Much more movement in the whole body: a stamp of the foot, the sulk shown the way the reference video shows it | Passed to its session |
| [Test Flight](../workshop/emotes/test-flight.md) | "The paper airplane is extremely small. We've kind of kept a momentum of what the paper airplanes look like size-wise everywhere else, yet for some reason they're way smaller." And: "I saw the paper airplane flap its wings like it was a butterfly ... They're supposed to just ... pick it up and throw it, and then it fly around, and then them catch it." | The plane is the size the paper planes are in the other emotes. It never flaps or wobbles like a bird: a rigid paper plane, thrown, flying its path, caught | Passed to its session |
| [Sneaky Toss](../workshop/emotes/sneaky-toss.md) | "Sneaky toss isn't bad. It's very close ... Honestly, I think sneaky toss is good. No, sneaky toss is good." | [Doodler](../workshop/emotes/doodler.md) | "I couldn't see it there because the first place one was one that hit the camera." The scene now tells a runner-up emote how long it can be seen (`kit.seenFor`, [the emote module](../workshop/emote-pipeline.md)) |
