# The winners' lighting: lamps out, curtain and cone in, 2026-11-20

## What Alan asked for
Each winner to light up at their own spotlight, with Camera Flick as 1st's emote (Dev: hangar, F8, "end: flick").

## Alan's Play notes, in order
- **Real lamps, brightness 5:** "the character models are still too dim, it's like the light isn't even lighting
  them up."
- **Real lamps, brightness 15:** the light lagged behind the spotlight and "was pulsing from lighted OK to too dim".
  His idea: a false spotlight, and light the models by other means.
- **Curtain, no lamps:** a frame where the winners "slightly lit up" before the lights (Claude had missed it); and
  the spotlight "is just a 2D light image... looks very bad"; he wants a cone that envelops the character.
- **Cone around the winner:** make the cone artificial light again and give the models the lighting they had before
  it; the light must not extend over the desk; and, once, the plane kept its red outline through the dark and in
  1st's hand.
- **Cone behind the winner:** "It almost looked good", but patches of light lay beside the books ("what is this?");
  the books stood on the desk's edge (1st's "aren't even on the desk"); the models popped in during the glide;
  2nd "doesn't even really feel like they're in the cone".
- **After those fixes:** "it's good now", but 2nd and 3rd were on the border of the view; camera a little further
  back. Then: "framing is good now."
