# Playtest 2026-10-17: menus, loading screen, fold credit

Alan's notes from playing (not at the PC for most of the day). How each will be checked is in brackets; ✅ = done.

1. **Menu board pictures are out of date:** the pinned pictures on the menu boards show an older game. Replace them
   with pictures of the game as it is now. ✅ [Studio captures of today's town; Codex drew the two crayon drawings
   from them (bell tower with the monorail; the town from above) and a "cinematic" version of a third for the photo
   (Alan picked it over the plain screenshot), set into the old photo frame. Checked on the menu in Play.]
2. **AI-looking pictures:** rulers in the menu art have gibberish numbers. Players read obvious AI art as low
   effort, so every ruler (and any other text-like marks) must read as real measurements, or be retouched out. ✅
   [Main menu now reads 1-7, settings 3-11 and 4-8 (its two edges), results 4, 6, 7, 10 and 18; the hangar ruler
   already read 1-6. The made-up numbers were erased and real ones lettered in at each ruler's angle.]
3. **Loading screen plane:** the paper plane slides across without turning, so it looks like it floats. Turn it to
   face the way it flies. ✅ [Loading: it flies across nose first, no pin. Menu: it flies in to its spot and only then
   a pushpin pops in on its nose. The plane picture was retouched (its painted pin and dashed trail taken out, full
   nose kept), so neither screen crops it any more; the waiting screen shows the same clean plane.]
4. **Loading screen words:** always the same line. Rotate through classroom lines that fit the game: the other
   players are setting up the classroom, colouring the pages, folding their planes, and so on. ✅ [12 lines, a new one
   every 2.5 s.]
5. ✅ **"This picture didn't load" shows every time** before the game starts. [Roblox reports some pictures as failed
   even when they show; the note helped nobody, so it's gone (the count stays in a debug attribute).]
6. **Fold credit and assists:** who gets the fold when one pilot did 80% and another finished it with 20%, and how
   long old damage should count; can a finisher "steal" a fold; does a repair wipe earlier damage? Look at how
   other games do it. ✅ [Alan chose: big damage wins, +25 finishing shot, 15 s window, repair wipes old damage
   ([decision](../decisions/2026-10-17-fold-credit-big-damage-wins.md)); 321 tests. Popup reads FINAL TEAR (Alan).]
7. **Two hangars confuse new players:** editing mid-match means Menu → Hangar, and nobody knows whether edits reach
   the quick hangar or how to fly them. [Research: games with saved loadouts put Edit next to Select (Call of Duty
   class select, Battlefield 2042 deploy screen, War Robots decks), keep one list of builds everywhere, and make
   "pick" and "save over" look different (Hunt: Showdown). Found 5 bugs: the hangar always opens on build 1;
   clicking a build there silently switches the flown build and drops unsaved edits; the quick hangar can highlight
   the wrong build; the desk shows the current plane's stats, not the picked build's; refold charges even for the
   same build. Alan chose the desk flow ([decision](../decisions/2026-10-17-builds-change-at-the-desk.md)).]
8. **Hangar flow works** (Alan, evening): the desk Edit / Save & fly changes are good.
9. **Click a weapon again to clear it:** on the weapons tab, clicking the weapon already on the picked mount takes
   it off. Click once adds, click again clears. The button stays for kids who miss that, renamed "Clear weapon spot"
   (Alan: words must be easy for a young audience).
10. **Death message:** "Destroyed. New plane in 4 seconds" is dull. Rotate fresh, on-theme lines (the plane got
    shredded, one fold too many...; Alan gave the idea, not the words) and say the plane is being refolded instead of
    a number of seconds. Alan widened it: every event message follows this
    ([decision](../decisions/2026-10-17-fresh-rotating-lines.md)). [Death text: src/server/FlightService.luau `destroy`.]
11. **Discuss after 9-10: a game that never ends?** Instead of waiting between rounds, the next round starts while
    everyone keeps flying; results show after each mode ends. Alan also sees the case for a pause to read results,
    XP and the next unlock. Bring research and a recommendation; Alan decides.
    **Discussion so far (2026-10-17):** Alan likes the TF2-style "keep playing" idea. XP, level and next unlock move
    to the main menu board and the hangar (reachable mid-match through Menu without leaving). His idea: the round
    end is announced in-game, vote targets drop from the ceiling in front of the desks on both sides, and you vote
    by shooting one; that can double as the results. Worries: results while flying means crashing; and he wants a
    winners' moment (top 3, or the winning team's best, jump on their desks and emote). Decided and first pass
    built; Alan's notes on trying it (cutscene, results detail, rings, dev button) are in the
    [decision](../decisions/2026-10-17-rounds-never-stop.md).
