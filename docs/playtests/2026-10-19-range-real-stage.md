# Playtest 2026-10-19: the Hangar Range's real stage

Alan looked at the Range built from its art (Edit previews and his own Play) through the evening. What he said and
what changed. Decision: [row picker, still camera, aim pick](../decisions/2026-10-19-range-row-picker-and-aim-pick.md).
System: [tutorial](../systems/tutorial.md).

## Stage
- First Edit preview: the cloud cloth's edges showed and the town was small. **Done:** bigger cloth pulled down, bigger town.
- First Play: "we're just in a big black box". **Done:** the view stays on the stage; drapes, floor and bulbs round it.
- "You can see the stage just floating": **Done:** one cardboard floor runs from the stage back under the plane.
- "When I turn my camera, the cardboard cutouts move and change": **Done:** still camera; the crosshair moves instead.

## Plane and aiming
- "Your plane needs to stay level": **Done.**
- The near targets stole the aim marker from the far ones: **Done** (whole game): the marker follows the nearest lead.
- "I can't hit any of the targets that change the mode": the plates were outside the aim limit. **Fixed** with the
  still camera. Then: the plane sat in the way and shots didn't meet the crosshair. **Fixed:** the plane hangs low at
  the bottom like the concept and points at what the crosshair is on (Play: 27 of 40 hits on a target 410 studs away).

## Row picker
- Shootable knock-down plates, "ting", stays down while picked; bigger plate = closer row. **Done.**
- He asked to see the plates assembled before uploading (I'd skipped it). Then: on the floor, 20% bigger, on sticks to
  their hinges, "Long" over "range" and bigger. **Done**, uploaded by Alan, in the game.

## Side board (asked, not built)
- Shoot its strips; difficulty = speed, size, then swoops; score resets on any change plus a reset strip; the Range
  keeps its own five loadouts, any part allowed, saved with the profile.
