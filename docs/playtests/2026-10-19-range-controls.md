# Playtest 2026-10-19 (late): the Hangar Range's controls

Alan judged Edit previews and played the Range three times through the late evening. What he said and what changed.
Decision: [controls and menu](../decisions/2026-10-19-range-controls-and-menu.md). System: [tutorial](../systems/tutorial.md).

## Layout
- Two side boards (stretched cardboard): "looks really bad", "taking up too much of the screen", targets only had a
  small patch. **Done:** boards gone; targets sweep wide and high; Codex's second opinion agreed (Gemini CLI no longer runs).
- Row picks inside a fold-out menu: "see how many steps that took?" **Done:** plates back on the floor, one click.
- Plate sticks stood behind their feet: **Done:** each stick stands on its hinge, one piece.
- Range Menu / Hangar notes in the way of shooting: **Done:** plain strips at the left edge, Hangar hanging from Range
  Menu, strings behind the paper. Not yet seen in Play.

## Controls
- "The crosshair should act as a cursor" (Range only). **Done.** Also clicks the game's Menu button.
- Menu should drop on strings with a bounce and pull back up. **Done;** it stuck halfway 3-4 times with a tween, now a spring.
- Loadout names: "how am I going to know what that is?" **Next session:** the Range's own loadouts with names.

## Bugs found
- Nose dot off the aim circle and drifting when still. **Fixed** (drawn at the aimed spot).
- The Range acted as a lesson: K skipped to a match. **Fixed** (a room, no note).
- Loadout swap: the plane flew off and never came back. **Fixed** (network ownership given back after a refold).
- Long-range shots never landed, ring never orange, dot never red. **Fixed:** targets were hit as the side-on plane
  under the card; now as the card. Nose aims at the lead ring's distance.
- Maneuvers "just impossible". **Done:** Challenge speed plus a smaller weave; Veteran 1.4x.
