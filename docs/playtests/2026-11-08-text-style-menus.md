# 2026-11-08 (late night): text style C in the menus

## What Alan did
Looked at text style C in the menus and the Hangar binder (Studio Play), first with the full look on every word, then
with loud and quiet words.

## What he saw
- The full look on every word was too strong: descriptive words (stat names, gear details, help text) were hard to
  read. Liked it on buttons and titles (Play, Hangar, Settings, Training, Launch, Repair, Fresh, Refill, Customize,
  Menu, Keep flying, Loadout names, the HUD's mode and timer strip).
- The build-name box had no style; it should be loud.
- The Hangar's stat names didn't fit their labels: not centred, too big ("too much in my face").
- Then: the real reason the text looks bad is the art behind it: stretched, low-resolution pictures with soft edges
  (the Back button's tack is squashed), so nothing pops.

## What changed because of it
- Loud and quiet words, the styled name box, centred stat names (built;
  [decision](../decisions/2026-11-08-text-style-crayon.md), [menus and HUD](../systems/menus-and-hud.md)).
- Next session, first: crisp menu pictures (his call), then the unlock moment.
