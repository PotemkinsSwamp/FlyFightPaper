# 2026-11-16: the eight Commons and the Dragon's boosters

## What Alan did
Judged each skin first on Blender previews of real planes (`.planning/skins/tests/`), then looked at them on planes in
Studio, on a build with the blunt nose.

## What he saw
- **Dragon's boosters** (green body scales): "its good".
- **Two ways test** (Carbon Fibre drawn in code vs TextureCraft): he picked the code-drawn way.
- **Racing Stripe:** the body's raised ridge "covers the racing stripe ... it's cut the racing stripe in half and made it
  look thinner". Fixed (stripes run over the ridge and up the fin): "looks fine".
- **Flame Decal:** on the blunt nose the flame "wraps around the blunt nose oddly" (a dark cap on a flaming nose). Fixed
  (a flame-coloured face): "good".
- **8-Bit:** first pass "too much purple", "all the 8-bitness is at the bottom ... you can't really see it at the top,
  which is where it matters". Second pass (three blocks in four bright, no purple): "too much ... like rainbow". Third
  pass (half and half; pink, cyan, yellow, violet): "that looks good".
- **Carbon Fiber, Neon Grid, Tie-Dye, Jungle Camo:** "good" as first shown.

## What changed
All eight Commons are in the game's list with approved pictures ([planes page](../systems/planes-and-hangar.md),
[first skin set](../decisions/2026-11-16-first-skin-set.md)); Tiger Stripes and Gold Foil are off it; the Skins tab
scrolls. Not checked: the tests after the list change, Carbon Fiber's weave in motion, the scrolling Skins tab (no note).
