# 2026-11-18 Big skins: size on screen and the hitbox

**Who:** Alan, FFA match in Studio; measurements by Claude in Studio's Edit mode (rays through a 1280x720 chase view).

## What he saw
- Hot Rod on the Cardboard Gunship with Cardstock Wings: the spoiler (its two tall tail fins) filled the middle of the
  screen.
- Ninja against the same plane without a skin, same seat (his two screenshots): "the wingspan is way longer ... the
  wingspan should be the same"; "if the biggest plane takes up 7.7% of the screen, then all skins should take up 7.7%".

## What was measured
- Under the "never shorter or narrower" rule: plain 7.7% of the screen on the Toilet Roll Gunship with Broad Wings;
  Race Car 34%, Ninja 21%, Hot Rod 18%; skins up to 1.6 times the plane's target there and 2.2 times on Swift Fold
  with Swept Wings. Under equal target (outlines from above, side and behind added): still up to 16% there, because
  the Cardboard and Toilet Roll bodies' deep fuselage is most of their target and the flat skins grew wide to match.
- Under the rule he picked (as long or as wide as the plane, never bigger): 2.4-5% skinned against 6.8-7.7% plain;
  every skin exactly the plane's length on the three builds checked, 18-21 studs wide against 23-31.
