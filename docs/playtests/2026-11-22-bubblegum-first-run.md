# Bubblegum: Alan's notes on the first run

**When:** 2026-11-22, Alan on Claude's muted Play recording of the first build (`.planning/emotes/bubblegum-first-run-cut.mp4`; the winner was the bot `itsfinn`, the wide body with the big hat). How the build works: [emote making](../workshop/emote-making.md).

## What he said, and what changes
| Alan | What changes |
|---|---|
| "It should be opaque to the point where you can only kind of see the silhouette of the character ... you can't see their face, because if you can see their face, then you can see them not actually blowing a bubble, which doesn't work well ... It shouldn't be that translucent." (He began with "a little too opaque" and corrected himself.) | The bubble gets much less see-through: the kid behind it is a shape, the face cannot be made out. Not built. |
| "the final bubblegum scene, it doesn't look like bubblegum stretched out. It looks like a bunch of bubblegum ... it should be kind of like one layer of bubblegum. And then the whole layer should kind of like stretch off, but like how bubblegum is sticky, so it's like it's being ripped off." | The lumps on the lens go. One layer of gum covers the lens and comes off as one piece, stretching as sticky gum does when it is pulled off. A plan in words first; not built. |
| "the stance, there's no movement in the stance. The whole point was they were supposed to like move, like readjust. Oh, I guess they kind of do. Yeah, it's kind of just the bubblegum that's the problem." | Nothing. The body (the stance, the weight shift, the lean) stays as it is. |
| Asked whether the rip is "pulled down, stretching into strands at the top that snap" or peeled from a corner, and whether the bubble may be nearly solid although 2nd and 3rd then vanish behind it for its last second: "yes pulled down, and yes make it nearly solid" | The plan he OK'd: at the pop one flat, smooth sheet of pink covers the picture (no lumps, no seams, a soft shine); it sticks about a second; then it is pulled off downward as one piece, its top edge stuck a moment longer so it stretches there into a handful of uneven strands, fat at the sheet and thin at the top, which thin and snap one after another. The bubble nearly solid pink: the kid a darker shape behind it. Not built. |

## Not said
- The speed: he raised no complaint on this recording (the first video was "a little sped up").
- The camera staying still while the bubble swells to the lens, and the report card losing 0.6 s.
- Whether the bubble should sit somewhere other than right over the face.
- Sound: after the look is right.
