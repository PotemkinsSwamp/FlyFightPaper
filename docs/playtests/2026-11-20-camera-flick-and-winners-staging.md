# Camera Flick and the winners' staging, 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20

## What Alan asked for
The winners' moment restaged (lights out, glide in the dark, spotlights 3rd, 2nd, 1st, book podiums) and Camera Flick
as 1st's emote, to see "what the whole winners animation could look like", behind a Dev button he presses himself
(hangar, F8, "end: flick").

## Alan's Play notes, in order
- **Staging:** not dark enough ("you should only really see their silhouettes"); the winners were sitting; the book
  stacks must be single books we stack; the zoom in on 1st must go ("they end up coming out of frame"); 1st's podium
  must look taller than 2nd's. All built.
- **Camera Flick, first cut:** "the flick is too fast": something has to happen first; the kid smugly looks down and
  admires the plane, then looks at the camera and throws.
- **With the admire:** the hand stayed still while the plane turned by itself in it; and the throw was a baseball
  throw, not how a paper plane is held.
- **With the hand turning it:** "that does not give off smug. It gives off rushed, hurried." Slow, with stops.
- **Slow cut:** the arm was wrong (hand too low, head too far down, forearm twisted the wrong way) and words were not
  getting it across. He will not film or photograph himself. Six numbered stills settled it: number 2's hand height,
  starting on the plane's bottom with the elbow tucked in, then elbow out and forward to its top.
- **Picked arm:** "now he's holding it correctly and throwing it correctly", but the plane sinks into the hand.
- **Resting on the hand:** on a thin-armed avatar the plane floated under the hand (his picture). The plane must sit
  snugly in the hand on every body without per-body tuning; the hand's own turn need not be right. He also asked why
  everything in the scene looked low-resolution.
- **Real models, first try:** the room went dark, the camera moved over and back, and nothing else happened (my bug:
  a name clash crashed the winners' set-up every frame).
