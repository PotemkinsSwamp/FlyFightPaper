## Aim
- Close and medium hit well; the long row was "almost impossible" side to side, fine up and down, with the green dot
  in the orange circle. **Cause (measured in Studio):** shots went to the crosshair, but the dot shows the nose, which
  trails the crosshair by 1-2 degrees while tracking: 5 studs off at close, 10 at medium, 24 at long (a card is 13
  studs from middle to edge). Dot on the ring hit 100% / 82-90% / 2-44%; crosshair on the ring hit 100% everywhere,
  and the dot was never red then. The ring itself was right.
- **Done (Alan's pick):** shots follow the dot (the nose), as in a match; the crosshair is what you steer with.
  Re-measured: dot on the ring hits 100% on every row at Challenge, Veteran and Maneuvers, pellets and pencils.
