# Alan's playtest notes — 2026-10-13 (evening)

Alan's overall verdict: sounds are pretty good; death animations are pretty good; weapon placing is better.
Each note: what Alan asked, then how it will be checked. Alan's word is final on these. ✅ = done (fill in as work lands).

## Deaths and the camera
1. ✅ (look pending) **Shredding looks like the plane tearing apart:** pieces of paper drift down slowly and keep falling for a few
   seconds; they vanish before reaching the ground. Check: Play capture of a shot-down plane, pieces airborne ~3–4 s.
2. ✅ **The camera follows your remains until you respawn**, for both deaths (paper ball and shreds). Today a crash into a
   building leaves you staring at the building. Your "entity" is the paper ball / the shreds until respawn.
   Check: crash and get shot down in Play; camera tracks the ball/shreds the whole time.
3. ✅ (look pending) **Remains keep your team colour:** a red plane crumples into a mostly-red paper ball, a blue one mostly blue;
   shreds include the plane's colour, so it reads as the plane itself shredding. In free-for-all (split planes, note
   19) the paper ball and shreds mix red, blue and white (Alan). Check: Play capture, red, blue and free-for-all.

## Flight
4. ✅ **Camera jitter when Lift runs out** (a glitch): the camera shakes as if trying to catch up with the plane.
   Fix the cause. Check: run out of Lift in Play, smooth camera (frame-to-frame offset steady).
5. ✅ **Momentum from diving also works with Lift left** (it's physics): dive to build speed, zoom back up. With Lift the
   payoff must not be too big (Lift shouldn't stack into a huge boost); the math needs balancing. Check: dive and
   pull up with and without Lift; speed gain with Lift is real but bounded.
6. ✅ **Fan gust gives Lift for as long as you're in it**, continuously (no timer: today you get ~20 once, then nothing
   on a second pass), and pushes you up harder, like a real fan. Staying alive forever by using fans is fine.
   Check: fly through, turn, fly through again; Lift rises each pass and the longer you stay.

## Sounds
7. ✅ (ear check pending) **Whoosh only when faster than your plane's normal flight:** normal flying (even the fastest plane) has no whoosh,
   just the flight sound. Whoosh rises with speed above normal: diving, speed power-ups, boost. A slow plane at its
   top dive whooshes less than a very fast plane going faster. Today it plays all the time.
   Check: cruise = silent; dive / power-up = whoosh that grows with speed.
8. ✅ (ear check pending) **Pencil launcher:** a rubber-band "thwap", then the pencil sound; the pencil sound (current one) much louder,
   it's barely audible.
9. ✅ (ear check pending) **Stapler:** a little quieter (just a small bit).
10. ✅ (ear check pending) **Precision paperclip:** can't hear it. Wants a subtle but audible electric "zzz" (it's a railgun) then a distinct
    metal ting.
11. ✅ (ear check pending) **Equal loudness:** all weapons about the same volume, audible both when launched and flying through the air;
    the guided eraser missile and the pencil launcher louder (they're rockets).
12. ✅ (ear check pending) **Hearing range:** you shouldn't hear everyone's weapons; other planes' weapons only within a set range.
13. ✅ (ear check pending) **Being shot at:** a subtle paper-tear sound when shots pass close / hit you.

## Weapons
14. ✅ **Every weapon fires the instant you click** — today the pencil comes out about a second late. Check: shot
    appears on the click frame for all six weapons.
15. ✅ **Stapler burst cooldown 0.8 s** (now about 1.2–1.5 s).
16. ✅ (look pending) **Every projectile is visible in flight.** The paperclip is too small to see: give it a metal shine/glint so you
    can see it coming. Check: Play capture of each weapon's shot at combat distance.

## Bots
17. ✅ **Bots can't see behind them or read your aim.** Today they seem to know where you're pointing and dodge your
    trajectory. They may know they're being shot at (shots hitting / passing them) and do normal maneuvers to get
    behind you, but no eyes in the back of their head and no reading where you're about to fire.

## Plane colours (ruling, 2026-10-13)
18. ✅ **Team deathmatch:** your plane is your team's colour: blue team = blue with circles, red team = red with squares.
19. **Free-for-all (Alan picked from two options):** every plane split down the middle — left half red with a square,
    right half blue with a circle. (Not plain white: white faded into the cream room in the photos.)

## Hangar weapons (make it work for young kids)
20. ✅ **Smart placing:** if you pick a weapon that doesn't fit the selected mount (e.g. a heavy weapon while a medium
    mount is selected) and there's capacity, it goes to a mount that fits, and whatever was there moves to another
    spot that fits it. No "no" message when a fix exists.
21. ✅ (look pending) **Show what can't fit:** when no mount can take a weapon (no heavy mount on this body, or every fitting mount is
    full / no capacity), its card is crossed out like a crayon X, and says why (e.g. "no heavy mount", "make room
    for a heavy"), so a kid sees what to change.
