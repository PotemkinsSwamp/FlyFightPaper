# 2026-11-13: Printed-sheet skins, first look in Studio

## What Alan did
Played in Studio with the two rough test skins (Hazard stripes, Lava cracks) on the printed-sheet piece copies
([skins plan](../plan/SKINS-PLAN.md)): tried them on the regular plane with the "Dev: skin" button and looked at the
bots wearing them; before that, the Blender renders (`.planning/skins/sheet/starter/review.jpg`, `heavy/review.jpg`).

## What he saw
- **"The skins actually look pretty good."** For Commons and Rares, "the skinning is going to work pretty well".
- He'll try a really stretched build himself to see how they look.
- **Boosters are part of the plane:** they take the red or blue team colour, so they must wear the skin too.
- **A Skins tab and a Trails tab in the customization menu,** so once everything is picked you see what the skin looks
  like on your plane.
- **Runtime picture memory:** when told the runtime look builder needs in-memory pictures, he pushed back ("other
  phone games ... have detailed skins, so we must be doing something wrong"). Answer: that route was dropped the same
  session; skins use ordinary uploaded pictures on ready-made looks (`tools/art/skin_looks.py`), the standard way.
