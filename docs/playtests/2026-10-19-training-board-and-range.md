# Playtest 2026-10-19: Training board and the Hangar Range

Alan played the Training board and the Hangar Range's rough build in Studio. What he said and what changed.
Decisions: [Hangar Range and Hangar Tour](../decisions/2026-10-19-range-map-and-hangar-tour.md). System: [tutorial](../systems/tutorial.md).

## Training board
- The first board (the menu's leftover pieces) looked bad: he wanted a real bulletin board, each lesson on its own
  pinned paper with Play under it, the title on a taped sheet. **Done:** new art from concept B/C, uploaded by Alan.
- Match Tips off the board, the Range on it; no "Coming soon". **Done.** "It looks good. I have no problems with it."
- Leaving a lesson (Menu > Leave match) kept pulling him back in, also when picking another lesson. **Fixed:** Leave ends
  the lesson and goes to the menu; switching lessons clears the old one first.
- The lesson's dim and spotlight covered the menu. **Fixed:** they hide while a menu is open.
- No 10-minute limit on the Training Room ("people will leave, or they'll be kicked for being idle"). **Done.**

## Hangar Range (rough build)
- Concepts: a puppet-theatre stage, not a cardboard box; our town as cardboard scenery; the theatre fills the view;
  bigger and deeper with close and far rows; plane smaller; more tear steps; red and blue targets only. **In the plan.**
- Rough build: "Mechanically it works good, conceptually. It just needs the art and the rest of the buttons." Its name
  is **Hangar Range**. **Renamed.**
