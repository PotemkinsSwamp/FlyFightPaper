# Weapons Range and Hangar Tour: how other games do it

Search quality was patchy. Where I could not confirm something, I say so.

## Ranges in shooters

- **Apex Legends Firing Range** is the best model. Since Season 16, dummies can strafe side to side at adjustable speeds, crouch, do both at random, and have shields raised up to red. Ammo is infinite only if you turn on an "infinite reloads" setting. A stats bar at the top shows shots fired, damage, headshots, kills and accuracy %. Hit markers stay on screen for a few seconds so you can read a gun's recoil pattern. Respawn called these "the first of several" rounds of updates. https://www.dexerto.com/apex-legends/apex-legends-firing-range-gets-major-update-in-season-16-2050310/
- Season 17 added a bigger map, a 1v1 Duel Pit, a movement course, and dummies that "run, strafe, and fight back" with adjustable difficulty. Friends can also join a running session. https://www.dexerto.com/apex-legends/apex-legends-firing-range-gets-massive-overhaul-in-season-17-with-new-map-1v1-pit-movement-practice-2128703/
- EA's help page confirms the range has Dummy Movement (stand, strafe, random), stance (stand, crouch, random), a Run speed, a Target Speed for background moving targets, and a shooting setting where dummies fire back ("Full Combat" is hardest). https://help.ea.com/dk/help/apex-legends/apex-legends/apex-legends-tips (taken from a search summary; the page timed out when fetched)
- **Valorant Range** has four practice modes. Open Range is free play. Shooting Test uses an F3 menu to set bot speed, streak, bot movement, armor and ammo. Speed is Easy, Medium or Hard. Streak spawns 50 or 100 bots. https://www.gamerevolution.com/?p=644326 and https://www.fragster.com/best-ways-to-warm-up-and-improve-in-valorant-using-the-range/ (the Fragster page does not list stats).
- **Overwatch Practice Range** has about 4 friendly and 11 hostile bots that stand still, shoot or move, with no difficulty dial in the base range (fan wiki summary). https://overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Practice_vs._AI Community "Workshop" ranges add difficulty from Very Easy to Very Hard, bot speed, strafe ratio, reaction time and fire-back delay. https://workshop.codes/ATJZQ A Workshop range also has buttons for damage boosts so you can practice timing. https://blizzardwatch.com/2019/12/16/overwatch-practice-range-2-0/
- **CS aim_botz** (community map) uses an in-map panel to toggle moving bots, random strafing, bot armor, bot HP (a guide suggests 10 for headshot warm-ups), and quick respawn. https://critfeed.com/how-to-play-aim-botz-in-cs2/ and https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/243702660
- **Call of Duty (MW2 2022)**: the Gunsmith has a "Go to Firing Range" button, so you test builds without risking your K/D. I could not confirm that you can swap parts mid-practice. https://www.charlieintel.com/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-2/modern-warfare-2-warzone-2-gunsmith-brings-firing-range-to-test-weapon-loadouts-181418/ Players say it was the first CoD where you could test guns against moving targets without leaving the lobby. https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/black-ops-6-underwhelming-firing-range-has-fans-demanding-an-apex-legends-glow-up
- **The Finals** puts loadout terminals in the arena and has a Shooting Gallery challenge of moving-dummy waves at Easy, Standard and Hard. https://thefinals.wiki/wiki/Practice_Range
- **ARC Raiders**: anything you test is free; ammo and durability do not carry out of the range. https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Practice_Range
- **Fortnite**: community islands, such as "Aim Practice Challenge", have moving targets and let you test different guns. These are not official ranges. https://www.fortnite.com/creative/island-codes/9388-0600-5158

## Ranges in flight games

- **Ace Combat Infinity "Test Flight"** is the closest match to our idea. A control tower walks you through each control (takeoff, turning, speed, missiles, target switching). Then you get a 5-minute free-flight phase with dummy air targets (16), rings (75) and ground/naval dummies, each worth 200 points. You can replay as often as you like. https://acecombat.wiki.gg/wiki/Test_Flight
- **War Thunder Test Flight** is a solo mode. Players say you cannot choose the targets in the default setup. The Mission Editor button lets you pick enemy type, how many, AI skill, and custom difficulty such as unlimited ammo. Slower targets are easier to practice on. https://forum.warthunder.com/t/test-flight/237148
- I could not find a real training mode for Project Wingman, World of Warplanes or Ace Combat 7. I found nothing either way, so I am not claiming they have none.

## Roblox examples

- **Rivals** has a Shooting Range reached from the Weapons menu with "Try out for free!" on any locked weapon. It has a red 150-HP person dummy that respawns and purple/yellow dummies with unlimited health. Numbers above the dummy show total damage and DPS. It does not move or shoot back, and trying a gun does not unlock it. https://pixeltwelve.com/guides/roblox-rivals-shooting-range-test-weapons
- Rivals' beginner advice is to use the free four-slot loadout and practice in the range before queuing. https://bloxodes.com/articles/rivals-beginner-guide
- **Phantom Forces, Arsenal, Bad Business**: I found nothing confirming a range in any of them (the searches returned no relevant sources).
- Roblox flight games found (A-10 Warthog Airstrike Training, Plane Havoc, AeroFlights) mention training areas or target practice but I could not confirm details. https://www.roblox.com/games/13023306248

## Target patterns and difficulty

- Real ranges set difficulty with a few simple dials: movement type, speed, health/armor, and whether targets shoot back (Apex, Valorant, aim_botz sources above).
- Aim Lab splits each skill into two phases: first a static or precise phase, then a dynamic or erratic one. That is a nice template for "Noob then Veteran". https://aimlabs.com/articles/aimlabs/introduction-to-aim-training-the-basics-of-aimlabs/
- KovaaK's lets you change target size, speed and complexity. https://www.oneesports.gg/gaming/aim-lab-vs-kovaak/
- Some Aim Lab tracking tasks have no miss penalty, so you can hold fire. https://aimlabs.com/articles/?p=4501
- Fortnite's "Training Island V6" uses random, unpredictable tracking paths. https://www.fortnite.com/creative/island-codes/5630-7611-9409

## Feedback and stats

- Apex: live stats bar and hit markers that linger (Dexerto link above). The stats bar can be reset by holding the interact button since Season 17. https://forums.ea.com/idea/apex-legends-bug-reports-en/firing-range-dynamic-stats-interaction-bug/13559306
- Rivals: damage and DPS numbers over the dummy.
- Aim Lab shows a 6-point summary after each task: score, kills, % hit, time to kill, kills per second, accuracy. KovaaK's shows damage, accuracy and score versus earlier runs. https://www.oneesports.gg/valorant/aim-lab-vs-kovaak/
- Roblox's own advice: give feedback on every action (VFX, damage numbers, health bars) or players think nothing happened. https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques

## Weapon/loadout switching

- Rivals: leave and re-enter from the weapon menu, which is slow. The Finals: terminals inside the arena. Apex: your normal inventory.
- Rivals' guide says compare weapons from the same spot, at the same distance, on the same dummy. https://pixeltwelve.com/guides/roblox-rivals-shooting-range-test-weapons
- I found no source on one-key weapon switching or locked-part trials beyond Rivals.

## Hangar onboarding

- Roblox's guide names three tools: visual cues (spotlight the exact button, dim the rest, arrows), contextual tutorials triggered when the player can act on them, and timed hints for players who stall. Delay non-essential teaching to later sessions. Do not show a tutorial before the player can act on it. End onboarding with a reward moment. https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques and https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding
- A hint should wait until most playtesters would already have found the answer. Roblox's example used 11 seconds. Show each hint once.
- A practitioner guide suggests capping tooltips at three per session. It is a single opinion. https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@hegemonart/get-design-done@1.60.4/reference/onboarding-progressive-disclosure.md
- Roblox's creator docs advise reaching core fun within about five minutes and avoiding long locked tutorials. https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/engagement
- Ace Combat's Test Flight is a good flow: guided controls first, then free practice. (Link above.)

## Kid-friendly notes

- Roblox says younger players are often reading-averse. Use consistent icons and keep UI visual. Teach without words (arrows, particles, glowing trails). https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox
- For ages 8 to 12, the (older) education page suggests a large, minimal GUI that can be used one-handed, with no key information only in text. https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/planning-for-educational-settings
- Check font size and contrast on a phone. https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/adaptive-design

## Recommendations for our game (ranked)

1. **Use Apex's four dials**: target movement (still, circle, figure-eight, random), speed, size, and "shoots back" (off by default). Difficulty presets just set all four.
2. **Name the presets Noob / Challenge / Veteran / Maneuvers.** Keep Maneuvers as the random, jinking one. (Aim Lab's static-then-dynamic split supports the order.)
3. **Keep a live stats bar**: shots, hits, accuracy %, damage, targets down. Add a reset button, and a time-limited scored run (Ace Combat used 5 minutes) with best-score memory.
4. **Hit feedback on every shot**: hit marker, floating damage number, target wobble or tear, and a sound. Make the cut-out spin or fall when downed, then respawn.
5. **One-key weapon switching** (1 to 6 plus a wheel). Do not make players leave the range like Rivals does. Infinite ammo by default, with a setting to turn it off.
6. **Show a per-weapon mini-readout** (accuracy and DPS for the weapon you are holding) so kids can compare, as Rivals' DPS number allows.
7. **Make the Guided Eraser testable**: include some targets that fly away so lock-on has something to do. Add a slow target for Pencil Launcher.
8. **Put targets at several distances** (near, mid, far on the wires), since Apex expanded for "different distances".
9. **Start with a guided Hangar Tour in the Ace Combat style**: control tower voice, one step at a time, then free practice. Spotlight the exact button, dim the rest, use arrows not paragraphs.
10. **Keep the Hangar Tour short and skippable after the first time.** Each step should be an action (place a part, mount a weapon), not a screen to read.
11. **Add a timed hint** if a player stalls on a step, shown once.
12. **End the tour with a reward moment** and a "Go to the Range" button that drops them straight in.
13. **Rivals-style "try before you buy"** for locked weapons and parts, with a clear label "Trial only".
14. **A "Your loadouts" quick bar inside the range** to swap saved loadouts without leaving. This is our own idea, since I found no strong precedent.
15. **Kid UI rules**: big icons, minimal text, voice or picture hints, tested on a phone-size screen.
