# Audit, 2026-10-07

Three read-only reviews feed [MILESTONES](../plan/MILESTONES.md): a requirement-by-requirement code audit, an art-quality
investigation, and web research on Roblox best practice. This file keeps their findings so the builder has
file:line references. Labels: **SOLID** = plausibly shippable, **THIN** = sketch, **MISSING**, **WRONG** =
contradicts the docs or Roblox practice. Nothing here was verified by a human playtest.

**Pattern:** the rules and bookkeeping layer is strong and well tested. The parts a player feels are thin:
steering, shot reliability, visuals, audio, UI, bots and art. About 2,800 lines go to ledger, persistence and
commerce code. About 1,200 lines go to flight, combat and bots.

### WRONG
- **Steering is a rate joystick, not "the nose follows the mouse".** Mouse offset from the screen centre sets
  a yaw rate and an absolute pitch target. The camera is locked to the nose. This is the root of "wide turns"
  and overshoot. `src/shared/FlightRules.luau:32-42`, `src/client/FlightController.luau:29-39`,
  `src/client/ChaseCamera.luau:18-31`.
- **Failed movement validation kills the player.** A failed speed/distance check calls `destroy(...,
  "invalid_movement")`. That is a death and can credit an attacker. A 300 ms lag spike can trigger it.
  `FlightService.luau:311-314`, `FlightRules.luau:53-57`. Correct the position instead.
- **The mock shop grants Paper outside Studio.** `HangarService.luau:42-58` has no `IsStudio()` check, while
  the published server uses the real DataStore (`ProfileRuntime.luau:13-16`). That means free Paper if published.
- **The DataStore request budget is blown.** Each reward costs about 6 UpdateAsync calls, and upkeep costs
  7–11 calls per 30 s per player (`ProfileService.luau:728-746`). With 12 players that nearly fills Roblox's
  ~180 requests/min. It has never run on a real DataStore.
- **One profile failure stalls the whole server's round loop.** `RoundRuntime.luau:111-113, 182, 231`.
- **Ambience is a slowed footsteps loop, and the "music" slider controls it.** `Audio.luau:24-25`.
- **Instance attributes are used as a per-frame replication channel.** Lift is written every frame for every
  plane (`FlightService.luau:306`). Reload strings update at 10 Hz (`CombatService.luau:199-215`).

### MISSING
- Lag compensation for hits. Targets are tested at the server's current position, which is 15–25 studs off
  from what the shooter saw. This is the likely main cause of "unreliable shots".
- Locally predicted tracers. Tracers arrive after the server's 10 Hz batch plus ping (`CombatService.luau:244-247`).
- Bot personality independent of skill.
- Practice mode.
- Airspace Control HUD (zone marker, capture state).
- Live commerce. `CommerceService.luau` is never required, and there is no
  MarketplaceService/ProcessReceipt anywhere.
- Positional (3D) audio.
- Evidence of 60 FPS with 12 planes over the city.

### THIN (the important ones)
- Horizontal fan pushes along the plane's heading, not the fan's direction (`FlightRules.luau:44`). Only 3 fan
  volumes exist, and `config.world.fans` is never read.
- Out-of-bounds gives no warning before the edge (`FlightService.luau:307-310`).
- The collider is a fixed 14×2×15 times body scale, whatever parts are fitted (`Aircraft.luau:38`).
- Impact damage trusts client-reported velocity (`FlightService.luau:231-232`).
- Rubber bands fire at the same rate as pellets, so they aren't "rapid".
- Projectile visuals arrive late from the server muzzle. There are no impact effects and one flash
  instead of one per mount.
- Bot navigation is a fixed 7×5×3 grid from config, not baked from the city (`AirRoutes.luau:8-17`). The brain
  is one `decide()` function (`BotService.luau:69-141`) with a fixed sideways jink as "evade". Bot avatars are
  tinted default bodies, and bot loadouts ignore tier.
- Death effect is 8 white rectangles.
- Protection has no dome or sound, and warnings are only a text colour change.
- The paid refold has no confirmation step.
- Results, hangar, shop, HUD and menus are plain offset text labels with no UIScale.
- Nameplates show only within 320 studs in a 2800-stud room, and there are no off-screen indicators.
- The tutorial drops you into a live match with no practice space.
- The quality setting only changes the effects pool cap.
- Weapon positions use one generic formula, so weapons can clip wings (`Aircraft.luau:4-6`).
- Session locking has no lock stealing, and a quick server hop fails.

### Architecture verdict
| System | Build on it? | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Shared pure rules (Catalog, Economy, Match, BotRoster, Service, Combat, Flight rules, BoostClock) | Yes | Keep |
| Client steering and camera | No | Rewrite as mouse-aim (about 100 lines) |
| FlightService | Partly | Keep client ownership. Split the god object. Correct instead of kill. Stop per-frame attributes. |
| CombatService | Yes, core | Add predicted tracers, lag compensation, a per-weapon behaviour table, impact events and relevance filtering |
| BotService and AirRoutes | Partly | Keep avoidance. Rewrite the brain as a state machine with personalities. Bake the graph from the city. |
| MatchService and RoundRuntime | Yes, overbuilt | Fix the reward stall. Delete the unused APIs. |

Hygiene: magic numbers bypass config (120 protection, 14° lock cone, 320 nameplate range, 768 shot cap…).
Dead code: CommerceService, `publicProjection`, `config.world.fans`, the unused MatchService APIs.
Server/client files are dense one-liners that are hard to review. The 232 tests cover rules, not the game.

## 2. Why the art looks worse

The individual building models did **not** get worse. They match the old ones: 1024 px textures and about
4k–9.5k triangles each. The drop comes from:

1. **Content was cut.** The old scene had about 40 structures plus a 326-MeshPart detail layer
   (`Workspace/FlyFightPaperEditorPreview`). Commit `c46a7e7`/`4440a6f` deleted it. The city is now
   **9 buildings + 9 book stacks**. Desks, chairs, trees, shelves and lights became primitive Parts, while about
   **154 uploaded MeshParts sit unused in ServerStorage**: `DormantArt/SavedCityVisualBank` (56),
   `FlyFightPaperNativeAssets` (69), `FlyFightPaperPerimeterAssets` (9), `SavedElevatedCardboardBank` (6),
   `StaticOakGeometry460`, `SavedChalkboardBank` (12).
2. **The room grew 3×** (900×700×300 → 2800×2200×620) with no new buildings. About 98% of the floor is empty planks.
3. **The lighting is flat.** `GlobalShadows=false`, environment light 0 in the working copy, Bloom 0. There is
   no Atmosphere, Sky, ColorCorrection, SunRays or light instances, and a grey Part stands in for the outside view.
4. **No PBR maps.** Every surface is TextureID on SmoothPlastic, with zero SurfaceAppearance. Meshy's 2048
   normal and roughness maps were discarded.
5. **Weak textures on some models.** The `craft` atlas is 16 flat swatches (used by the tube tower and the gable
   house). `toy-bricks` has 107 colours. 11 of 12 chalkboard pieces are untextured.
6. **192 Neon landing segments** clash with the warm palette.
7. **Nobody looked.** The Studio viewport was 1×1, so no in-game screenshot was ever reviewed.

Raw Meshy assets (`assets/models/meshy*/`): 13 GLBs, 5.2k–9.8k triangles, full PBR at 2048
(colour, metal/rough, normal). Auto-UV atlases have many small islands, which blur when shrunk, so re-bake
onto clean UVs. `meshy561` has no GLB, and `meshy713` has maps only.

## 3. Research: Roblox best practice (sources in the research notes; [REC] = starting values to tune)

- **Mouse-aim flight (War Thunder / MouseFlight pattern):**
  - Lock the mouse to the centre and accumulate mouse movement into a free aim direction.
  - The camera follows the aim. The plane's autopilot steers toward the aim point:
    `local = plane:PointToObjectSpace(aim).Unit`. Yaw and pitch come from local x/y. Roll blends from
    wings-level to aggressive bank as the angle off target grows past about 10°.
  - Drive with `AlignOrientation` (OneAttachment, Responsiveness about 15–40, finite torque) and
    `LinearVelocity` with finite force.
  - Draw two reticles: the aim point and where the nose actually points.
  - References: https://github.com/brihernandez/MouseFlight and
    https://wiki.warthunder.com/Instructor/How_the_instructor_works
- **Camera:**
  - Scriptable camera with a spring or `TweenService:SmoothDamp`. Position should lag a little more than rotation.
  - Spherecast pull-in on collision.
  - FOV kick with speed, about +8–12 on boost.
  - Small trauma-based shake. Speed lines at high speed.
- **Shots:**
  - The shooter's client draws a tracer immediately.
  - The server validates rate, origin and nose cone, then relays cosmetics on an `UnreliableRemoteEvent`
    (batched, about 50 KB/s per client).
  - Hits are validated against a **position history ring buffer** (about 1 s at 20–30 Hz) at the shooter's
    view time, plus a line-of-sight raycast. This is Roblox's own laser-tag tutorial pattern.
  - Hitboxes about 1.3–1.5× the visual size.
  - Hit marker plus a tick sound, with a bigger kill confirm. Lead pipper on target.
  - FastCast is unmaintained; use a hand-rolled stepped Spherecast.
- **Bots:**
  - Lead, pure and lag pursuit.
  - States: Patrol, Chase, Attack, Evade (enemy within 30° of tail), Extend, Search, Avoid (override), Recover.
  - Whisker spherecasts: forward ≈ speed × 1.2–1.5 s, plus 4 at ±30°.
  - Waypoint graph baked from the real map, with edges validated by spherecast, and A* routing.
  - **Feed the steering point into the same autopilot the player uses.**
  - Skill [REC]: reaction 200–450 ms, aim cone 2–6°, lead accuracy 0.7–1.0, bursts 0.3–0.8 s, 120° FOV plus
    line of sight, decisions at about 10 Hz.
- **Visuals and performance:**
  - Budget: under 1,000 draw calls, under 1M triangles, 16.7 ms frames.
  - 20k triangles per MeshPart.
  - SurfaceAppearance PBR (OpenGL normals). 4K texture rendering shipped in January 2026, but default to 1024
    and use 2K for hero pieces.
  - Texture streaming is automatic.
  - Tiled MaterialVariants for cardboard, paper and book cloth instead of unique atlases.
  - Future lighting; sun through the windows plus SunRays; Atmosphere density about 0.25–0.35; Bloom 0.3–0.6
    (threshold about 1.5–2); ColorCorrection saturation +0.1–0.2. No DepthOfField or Blur in gameplay.
  - SpotLights for shadows; avoid shadowed PointLights.
  - `CastShadow=false` and collision/query off on decor.
  - Persistent landmarks if streaming is ever enabled.
- **Assets:**
  - Open Cloud `POST https://apis.roblox.com/assets/v1/assets` (Model accepts fbx/gltf/glb/rbxm, 20 MB;
    poll `/operations`). Images can't be updated in place.
  - For complex models the Studio 3D Importer is recommended (it creates SurfaceAppearance).
- **Meshy:**
  - Image-to-3D with 2K/4K texture costs 30 credits. Retexture costs 10. Remesh costs 5.
  - `target_polycount`, `enable_pbr`, `remove_lighting`.
  - Concurrency is 10 on Pro.
- **Retention:**
  - Get to the fun within about 60 s: spawn flying, three prompts, a weak bot crossing in front, first hit
    under 30 s, first kill under 90 s.
  - Rounds of 5–8 minutes, 3 or more per session, aiming for 19+ minute sessions (the top-5% Roblox bracket).
  - After every round: an XP bar, Paper earned and a preview of the next unlock.
- **Market:**
  - No prominent paper-plane dogfight game exists on Roblox.
  - Plane Crazy (a plane builder, about 7K concurrent) shows demand for building your own plane.
  - Paper-plane simulators spike and fade.
