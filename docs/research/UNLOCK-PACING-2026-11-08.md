# Unlock pacing research (2026-11-08)

How other games pace levels and unlocks, for [CLASSES-PLAN](../plan/CLASSES-PLAN.md) step 4c. Web research by a Sonnet
helper; sources are mostly player reports, so this is direction, not calibration. Back to [HOME](../HOME.md).

## What was found
- **No clean "matches to buy an item" figures** for any Roblox shooter (Arsenal, Phantom Forces, Rivals, Bad Business,
  War Tycoon): wikis give costs, not earnings.
- **Call of Duty** (levels only, no currency): even very fast players report 18-24 hours to the level cap.
- **War Thunder / War Robots** (unlock, then pay): the second gate is the real grind, tens to hundreds of hours for top
  items; players buy premium boosts to shorten it. It is the most complained-about part of those games.
- **Brawl Stars:** prices are a design choice (fixed coin rate), set to take several days per upgrade.

## Our numbers today
XP to the next level is 200 + 40 x (level - 1). At Alan's estimate of a real player (about 15 folds, roughly 1,100 XP and
650 Paper a match): level 15 after about 6 matches, level 50 after about 52 (7 hours); a 5,000-Paper item is about 8
matches.

## Suggested targets (the helper's judgment, for Alan's OK)
- First new item within matches 1-2.
- Level 15 after about 20-25 matches; level 50 after about 150-250.
- A mid-tier weapon about 6-10 average matches of Paper; top weapons and special planes 25-50.
- Slow one gate, not both: if levels slow down a lot, keep prices; otherwise players hit a wall.
- Sources: Activision progression blog, War Thunder wiki and forum, War Robots official post and Steam threads,
  Brawl Stars guides (links in the session that made this page; none were measured data).
