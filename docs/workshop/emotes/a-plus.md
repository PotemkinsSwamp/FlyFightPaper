# A+

A 1st-place emote that covers the camera. Made by the [emote pipeline](../emote-pipeline.md), in its own side session; the idea is on the
[plan](../../plan/WINNERS-CIRCLE-PLAN.md).

**The idea:** Holds up their report card and slaps it on the lens, where it becomes the real report card.

## Where it stands
Step 1: the shot card is written and waits for Alan's OK. Nothing made.

## Shot card
**The feeling:** smug show-off, "read it and weep". Slow and showy, chest out, chin up; then one sharp slap.

**The clock it lives in** (from `Cutscene.play`): the camera holds on the three winners 10.4 s after 1st's light, then
leaves; the real report card comes up as it leaves and gets its 10 s (`CARD`). A+ does not change when the camera
leaves, so the card keeps all 10 s.

**Sizes** are shares of the picture's height. The picture at the winners is at least 3.45 kid heights tall, so a card
0.35 kid heights tall (waist to chin) is about a tenth of it. The real card (one-player rounds) fills 83% of the
screen's height, on the right side (about 64% to 99% across), square.

**Choices for Alan (my pick first):**
1. **What the card in the hand shows.** **B (my pick):** its back has a big red-pen "A+" in a red ring, readable at a
   tenth of the picture; it turns over in flight, so the front lands. **A:** only the front (the real card), where the
   A+ is the small ringed grade in 1st's row and cannot be read from the stack.
2. **When it lands.** **"Slap, then gloat" (my pick, the table above):** lands at 7.8 s, 1st gloats beside it for
   2.6 s. **"Late slap":** everything slower, it lands at 9.9 s and the camera leaves 0.5 s later: nothing after the
   slap but the jolt.
3. **Landing.** **Skids into place (my pick):** lands turned about 10 degrees, a little left and low of the real card's
   spot, and skids square into it in 0.25 s, like a paper slapped down on a desk. **Straight in:** lands square, exactly
   in place.

**How the slapped card becomes the real one:**
- **Same place and size:** the flying card ends exactly over the real card's rectangle, worked out on the frame from
  where the real card sits on that screen, so it fits a PC, a wide window or a phone.
- **Same look:** its front is the real card itself: the same picture and words, copied from the results card (it is
  already filled with this match's results when the winners' moment starts), lit by nothing in the room, so the dark
  room does not darken it.
- **When:** at 10.4 s, when the camera leaves as always, the real card shows and the copy goes in the same frame. Its
  10 s start then, untouched. (Counting the copy, the card is on screen 2.6 s longer than usual.)
- **Team rounds** (two cards, Red left, Blue right): it becomes the card of 1st's team; the other comes up at the
  take-over as usual. My default.
- **Not known until seen:** whether the copy on a 3D sheet is sharp enough that the swap does not blink.

**The body:** none of the 12 Roblox emotes the game already uses (Godlike, Celebrate, Jumping Cheer, Greatest and the
runner-ups' dances) holds something up or throws; a body cut from the Gemini video, as Book Kicker and Bubblegum. The
card is fixed to the hand(s) on every body.

**Reused:** Book Kicker's camera jolt, its hiding of the name plates, its flight-to-the-lens sums; Camera Flick's
flight time (about 0.4 s). Art already loaded: the report card picture, its red ring, the card's own fonts. No new
picture, no upload but the clip and two sounds.

**New (Alan's OK on this card covers them):** a copy of the real card in the kid's hand; the take-over at the camera's
leaving. **From the primary session later:** the emote hook, a way to find where the real card sits, and the name
plates hidden from the landing.

**Design check** (design-theory; on words now, on a screenshot at step 4): red pen (200, 38, 38) on the cream back is
about 4.4 to 1, over the 3 to 1 for big lettering (L9); the back has one loud thing, the A+ (1.3); the real card is
not changed (it keeps all its content).
