# Skeet Shoot

A 1st-place emote that uses all three winners. Made by the [emote pipeline](../emote-pipeline.md), in its own side session; the idea is on the
[plan](../../plan/WINNERS-CIRCLE-PLAN.md).

**The idea:** The runner-ups throw their paper planes and 1st shoots them out of the air, like skeet shooting, with rubber bands off a finger or a slingshot: something a child would do (Alan). The runner-ups' own emotes pause while they throw, then carry on. It does not touch the camera; how it ends is open.

## Shot card
**The feeling:** a cocky sharpshooter. 1st is cool and lazy, it's too easy: two shots, two hits, blows the smoke
off. The runner-ups are keen helpers: quick, eager throws. In the movement: 1st's moves are slow and loose, the aim
is dead still, only the shot itself is sharp; the second shot is taken without looking. The runner-ups move fast
and snappy, then drop straight back into their own emote.

**Finger, not slingshot (my pick, his call):** a finger gun with a red rubber band stretched along it. It needs one
hand only, so it works on any body (a slingshot needs the other hand to pull the band: two hands meeting, the risky
kind); the pointing hand reads as "shooting" at once; and blowing across the finger only works with a finger.

**The timeline** (seconds after 1st's light; 3rd's light came 2 s before, 2nd's 1 s before; my timings, about the
scene's usual 10.4 s, so the report card keeps its time). 2nd and 3rd stand on either side of 1st.

**What a hit looks like:** the plane jerks off its line, flips nose over once, and a puff of three or four tiny white
flecks (Nap's and Bubblegum's scraps). The band bounces off and drops out of sight.

**The ending, three versions for Alan to pick:**
- **A, the plan's idea (my pick): the delayed fall.** The hit planes don't drop at once: they wobble on, lopsided,
  sinking slowly in small circles. At 5.5 s 1st brings the finger up in front of the mouth and blows across it (head
  dips, shoulders drop). At 6.5 s both planes give up together and spiral down (1.5 s). Risk: plane 1 hangs on for
  almost 4 s, which could read as a miss before the joke pays off.
- **B, straight down.** Each plane spirals down the moment it is hit. At 6.0 s 1st blows across the finger, lowers
  it, and gives a small shrug ("easy").
- **C, the cowboy twirl.** As B, but at 6.0 s the finger gun spins once at the wrist like a cowboy's pistol, then
  the blow, then the hand comes down.

**Where the planes end up (my default):** they land on the desk top in front of 1st's book stack and lie there,
askew, until the scene leaves (a little trophy pile). The other way: they fall out of the bottom of the picture.

**At or over the lens:** nothing. The camera does nothing new.

**Roblox's own emotes for the body (checked in step 4):** the aim arm could start from Roblox's built-in Point
(`/e point`, free). I know of none for a throw or for blowing across a finger: I will search the catalog in step 4
and otherwise cut them from the Gemini video. The runner-ups' throw is an upper-body move laid over their frozen
emote, so it works standing (Good Game) or sitting (Nap, Next One).

**Reused:** the plane in the hand (`holdPlane`: a copy of each kid's own plane, sized as 1st's in Toy Pilot);
Nap's and Bubblegum's scraps for the flecks; Magician's spiral for the planes coming down; Bubblegum's way of timing
sounds to the clip's marks. **New:** the rubber band (plain parts, no picture), the throw, the shot.

**Sounds** (the music plays under them, so few and quiet): a short rubber-band snap at each shot (2.7 and 4.7 s), a
small dry paper tick 0.1 s later on each hit, and, quietly, the breath of the blow. No whoosh: nothing passes the
camera. Nothing for the planes landing.

**New mechanics, for Alan's OK:**
1. **The runner-ups' emotes pause.** Today 2nd's and 3rd's emotes never stop. With Skeet Shoot, each one's emote
   freezes where it is for about a second, the throw plays over it, then it carries on from the same spot. One at a
   time: 3rd, then 2nd.
2. **The scene hands their planes to this emote.** Today only 1st gets a copy of their own plane. The scene would
   make a copy of 2nd's and 3rd's planes too (the same way) and give them to this emote; each appears in its kid's
   hand while the hand is behind the back, and is thrown.
3. **Numbers:** every time above is mine and waits for his OK; the band's size and colour too.
