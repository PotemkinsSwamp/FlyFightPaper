# Skeet Shoot

A 1st-place emote that uses all three winners. Made by the [emote pipeline](../emote-pipeline.md), in its own side session; the idea is on the
[plan](../../plan/WINNERS-CIRCLE-PLAN.md).

**The idea:** The runner-ups throw their paper planes and 1st shoots them out of the air, like skeet shooting, with rubber bands off a finger or a slingshot: something a child would do (Alan). The runner-ups' own emotes pause while they throw, then carry on. It does not touch the camera; how it ends is open.

## Shot card (second version)
**The feeling:** a cocky sharpshooter. 1st is cool and lazy, it's too easy: two shots, two hits, a twirl. The
runner-ups are keen helpers: quick, eager throws. 1st's moves are slow and loose, the aim is dead still, only the
puff of the shot is sharp; the second shot is taken without looking. The runner-ups move fast and snappy, then drop
straight back into their own emote.

**The straw:** a long green straw (the green of the game's Spitball Straw), held in 1st's hand with one end at the
mouth. A prop on the hand, so it works on any body (on an odd body its end may sit off the mouth: the accepted loss
of 2026-11-21). The spitball is a small white ball; it sticks, as the game's spitballs do, and the plane goes down.

**The timeline** (seconds after 1st's light; 3rd's light came 2 s before, 2nd's 1 s before; my timings, inside the
scene's usual 10.4 s). 2nd and 3rd stand (or sit) on either side of 1st.

**Where the planes end up (my default, not yet answered):** on the desk top in front of 1st's book stack, lying
askew, until the scene leaves. The other way: they fall out of the bottom of the picture.

**How the runner-ups pause (his question):** an emote is a recording of joint turns played over time. The scene sets
that recording's speed to zero, which freezes the kid in the pose of that moment. Over it plays a short throw
recording that moves only the throwing arm, the chest and the head. Roblox lets the higher-ranked recording ("priority":
which one wins when two move the same joint) take over just the joints it moves; the legs and hips keep the frozen
pose. After the throw it fades out in a fifth of a second and the emote plays on at normal speed from the same frame.
**So a napping kid does not stand up:** it throws from where it sits. Nap's head jerks up awake, the throw, the head
sinks back (a small gag of its own). Good Game freezes mid-clap; Next One mid-fold. Standing up and sitting back
down would cost about two seconds each way and a new clip for every runner-up emote.

**At or over the lens:** nothing. The camera does nothing new.

**Roblox's own emotes for the body (checked in step 4):** I know of none for pulling something from the back, a
throw, a straw shot or a twirl; I search the catalog in step 4, else they are cut from the Gemini video.

**Reused:** the plane in the hand (`holdPlane`: a copy of each kid's own plane); Magician's spiral for the planes
coming down; Bubblegum's way of timing sounds to the clip's marks. **New:** the straw and spitball (plain parts, no
picture), the throw, the shot, the twirl.

**Sounds** (few and quiet, under the music): a short puff at each shot (2.7 and 4.7 s), a small wet tick 0.15 s later
on each hit. No whoosh: nothing passes the camera. Nothing for the planes landing or the twirl.

**New mechanics, for Alan's OK:**
1. **Three kids animated, the runner-ups' emotes paused** as above: 3rd's for about a second, then 2nd's.
2. **The scene hands their planes to this emote:** today only 1st gets a copy of their own plane; 2nd and 3rd would
   get one too, appearing in the hand behind the back.
3. **Numbers:** every time above is mine; the straw's size and colour too.
